## The short answer

- **Tripo** is the best overall choice for printing: fast, cheap per model, and it exports STL, OBJ and 3MF directly.
- **Meshy** is the easiest way to get a **multicolor print**, with a 3MF that opens in Bambu Studio with colors already assigned.
- **Rodin** captures the finest surface detail, good for figurines and display pieces, but its STL may need repairing first.

## How we evaluated this

## Who this is for

- **Gift makers** turning a pet photo or a child's drawing into a figurine.
- **Tabletop gamers** making custom miniatures and terrain.
- **Etsy and craft sellers** prototyping designs before modeling them properly.
- **New printer owners** who don't want to learn CAD yet.

AI generators are great for organic shapes. They are **not** the right tool for functional parts that must fit exact measurements, like brackets or replacement parts. Use CAD for those.

## 1. Tripo: best overall for printing

Tripo's printing workflow is simple: generate from a photo or prompt, then export as **STL, OBJ or 3MF**. Tripo says its meshes are watertight at generation, which saves repair work in the slicer. Independent testers have printed Tripo models from both photos and text prompts, including segmented multi-part prints.

**Why we pick it:** the lowest cost per printable model and broad format support. Paid plans start around $20 a month.

**Tips:** STL has no units, so check the size in your slicer. Downloads require a paid plan or trial.

## 2. Meshy: best for multicolor prints

Meshy has the most complete print toolkit. A printability check flags problems, auto-repair fixes many of them, and you set the real-world size before export. Its **Multi-Color Printing** mode reduces the texture to between 1 and 16 colors and exports a 3MF with filament slots pre-assigned for Bambu Lab's AMS.

Meshy also works with **MakerWorld**: Bambu Lab users can run Meshy's image-to-3D inside MakerLab and export straight to Bambu Studio.

**Tips:** a regular STL or 3MF download from Meshy is geometry only. For color, export through Multi-Color Printing.

## 3. Rodin: best surface detail for figurines

Rodin produces detailed surfaces and textures, and exports STL along with game formats. Hyper3D has also added print preparation features, including multicolor 3MF export with a preview of color regions.

**Tips:** reviewers recommend running Rodin's STL through a repair tool, such as Blender's 3D Print Toolbox or your slicer's repair function, before printing. The entry price is higher than Tripo or Meshy.

## Which file format should you export?

| Format | Color | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| STL | No | Single-color FDM or resin prints. Works in every slicer. |
| 3MF | Yes | Color or multi-material prints, and keeping units and parts together. |
| OBJ | Limited | Moving a textured model into Blender for editing first. |

## From photo to print: a checklist

1. **Pick a good image.** One subject, plain background, even lighting, front-on view.
2. **Generate a few versions** and keep the best one.
3. **Check it's watertight** using the tool's check or your slicer's repair function.
4. **Set the size** in millimetres. A 10-unit STL could be 10 mm or 10 inches.
5. **Thicken thin parts** such as tails, ears and swords, or they'll snap.
6. **Add supports** where the model overhangs, and orient it to hide seams.
7. **Print a small test** before committing to a large or multicolor print.

## Which printer do you need?

Any FDM or resin printer can print these files. For multicolor prints you need a multi-material system, such as a Bambu Lab AMS or a similar multi-filament unit. For tiny detailed figurines, resin printers give the sharpest results.

## FAQ

### Can I turn a photo into an STL file with AI?

Yes. Tripo, Meshy and Rodin can all generate a 3D model from a single photo and export it as STL. Expect to check and sometimes repair the mesh before printing.

### What's the best AI tool for multicolor 3D printing?

Meshy, thanks to its Multi-Color Printing mode that exports a 3MF with colors already assigned for Bambu Studio.

### Do AI 3D models print well?

Organic shapes like animals, figurines and props usually print well after a quick check. Models with thin parts, text or exact dimensions often need fixing or are better modeled in CAD.

### Is there a free AI tool to make STL files?

All three tools have free tiers, but downloads are limited. Tripo needs a paid plan or trial to download, and Meshy's free plan allows very few downloads.
