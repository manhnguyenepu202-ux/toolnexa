## Bot or no bot?

This is the first decision.

- **Bot-based tools** (Otter, Fireflies, Notta) send a visible participant into your call. Everyone sees it, which acts as a notice, but some clients find it off-putting.
- **Bot-free tools** (Granola, Krisp) capture audio on your own device. Calls feel normal, but other people may not know they're being recorded.

**Consent matters either way.** In 2026, class actions were filed against Granola, Otter and Fireflies over recording without everyone's consent. These are allegations, not findings. Whatever you choose, tell people at the start of the call that you're taking AI notes, especially when anyone joins from a state or country that requires all-party consent.

## 1. Granola: best bot-free notes

You type rough notes as usual. Granola listens to your computer's audio and, when the call ends, blends your notes with the transcript into clean, structured notes. AI additions appear in a different color, so you can see what came from you.

- **Price:** free Basic plan with limited history; Business about $14 per user per month, billed monthly only.
- **Platforms:** Mac and Windows for calls, plus phone apps for in-person meetings.
- **Watch out for:** the July 2026 class action over recording consent, and no annual discount.

## 2. Krisp: best for noisy calls

Krisp removes background noise and voices, then records and summarises the meeting without a bot. If you take calls from home, a café or an open office, it fixes two problems at once.

- **Price:** free plan with 60 minutes of noise cancellation a day; Core $16 a month or $8 a month billed yearly.
- **Watch out for:** support complaints, and only 7 days of note history on the free plan.

## 3. Otter: best cheap transcription

Otter is one of the oldest names in the category. You get a live transcript, summaries and action items, with a usable free tier.

- **Price:** free with 300 minutes a month; Pro $16.99 a month or $8.33 a month billed yearly, with 1,200 minutes and a 90-minute call limit.
- **Watch out for:** the bot joins by default, long calls need Business, and accuracy drops with jargon.

## 4. Fireflies: best for sales teams

Fireflies transcribes without a minute cap on any plan and pushes notes into CRMs. Business adds conversation analytics, useful for sales coaching.

- **Price:** free; Pro $10 per seat billed yearly ($18 monthly); Business $19 per seat billed yearly ($29 monthly).
- **Watch out for:** storage caps on Free and Pro, and advanced AI features that run on separate paid credits.

## 5. Notta: best for many languages

Notta transcribes 58 languages, handles bilingual meetings, and also transcribes uploaded files and phone recordings.

- **Price:** free with 120 minutes a month, capped at 3 minutes per recording; Pro about $8.17 a month billed yearly.
- **Watch out for:** many Trustpilot reviews describe charges after cancelling. Use a card you control and keep proof of cancellation.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI note taker without a bot?

Granola and Krisp both record from your own device, so no bot joins the call. Granola focuses on polished notes; Krisp adds noise cancellation.

### Is it legal to use an AI note taker?

It depends on where everyone on the call is. Some places require all participants to consent to recording. The safe habit is to announce that you're taking AI notes at the start of every call.

### What is the cheapest AI meeting note taker?

On yearly billing, Krisp Core, Otter Pro and Notta Pro all cost about $8 a month. Otter and Fireflies also have usable free plans.

### Which AI note taker supports the most languages?

Notta, with 58 transcription languages.
