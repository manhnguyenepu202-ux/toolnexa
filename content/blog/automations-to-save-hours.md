Automation tools like [Zapier](/tools/zapier) and [Make](/tools/make) turn repetitive clicks into background workflows. Here are eight we recommend to almost every small team.

## 1. New lead → CRM → welcome email

**Trigger:** form submission (website, Facebook Lead Ads, Typeform)
**Actions:** create contact in [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot), add to your email tool (e.g. [MailerLite](/tools/mailerlite)) with a tag, notify sales in Slack.

## 2. AI summary of inbound support emails

**Trigger:** new email in a support inbox
**Actions:** AI step summarises and classifies urgency, then creates a task with the summary.

## 3. Blog post → social posts

**Trigger:** new item in your RSS feed
**Actions:** AI step writes platform-specific captions, then schedules them in your social tool.

## 4. Meeting notes → task list

**Trigger:** new meeting transcript
**Actions:** AI extracts action items with owners, then creates tasks in your PM tool or a [Notion](/tools/notion) database.

## 5. Invoice paid → thank-you + review request

**Trigger:** payment succeeded in your billing tool
**Actions:** wait 3 days, then send a personal thank-you email with a review link.

## 6. Weekly KPI digest

**Trigger:** schedule (every Monday 9am)
**Actions:** pull numbers from your analytics, ads and CRM, format a digest and post it to your team channel.

## 7. Abandoned demo booking follow-up

**Trigger:** someone starts but doesn't finish a booking
**Actions:** add to a short follow-up sequence with a direct booking link.

## 8. New hire onboarding checklist

**Trigger:** new row in an HR sheet
**Actions:** create accounts checklist, schedule welcome meetings, share the onboarding doc.

## Zapier or Make?

- Choose **Zapier** if you want the fastest setup and the most app integrations.
- Choose **Make** if your workflows branch, loop over lists or run at high volume — it is usually cheaper per operation.
