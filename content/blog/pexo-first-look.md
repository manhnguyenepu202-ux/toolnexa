Pexo promises something appealing: describe a video in chat, and an agent handles the script, shots, voiceover, captions and edit. It might be very good. We can't tell you that yet, and neither can most of the reviews you'll find.

## Why this is a first look, not a review

We haven't scored Pexo, and that's deliberate. Trustpilot has just 15 reviews, we found no G2 or Capterra record, and none of the third-party reviews we read described real test generations. Two claimed testing in their headers or bylines, but gave no results. We'll publish a full review once there's enough evidence for a fair score.

## How we evaluated this

This is research only, without first-hand test videos of our own. We read Pexo's pricing page, terms, privacy policy and credit guide, its Trustpilot profile, and four third-party write-ups. Everything was checked on November 5, 2026.

## What Pexo is

Pexo calls itself a conversational AI video agent. You start from an idea, URL, PDF, image, video or audio file. It plans the video, picks models behind the scenes, then adds voiceover, captions, music and pacing. You mark what to fix and revise through chat.

The site names models including Seedance, Kling and Veo, and there are developer options too, including skills and a command-line tool.

## Who it's for

Pexo says it's built for product marketers, indie developers, creators, agencies and small businesses. On paper, the best fit is someone who wants a finished short video from a product page without learning an editor. It looks less suited to anyone who needs frame-level control, or a predictable cost per video.

## Pricing: what we can confirm

| Plan | Price | Base credits | Limited-time bonus | Pexo's estimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $30/mo | 3,000 | 1,800 | About 2 to 5 min |
| Elite | $60/mo | 7,200 | 2,800 | About 5 to 11 min |
| Max | From $100/mo | 14,000 | 4,000 | About 9 to 20 min |

Pricing checked November 2026.

Two things stand out. Those minute estimates are based on totals that include bonus credits. On Pro, 1,800 of the 4,800 credits are a limited-time bonus, so without it the estimate falls to roughly 1.25 to 3 minutes by simple proportion.

The bigger problem is that Pexo doesn't publish what a second of video costs. Its range implies $6 to $15 per finished minute on Pro with the bonus, and up to about $24 without it. That spread is too wide to budget from, so you'll only learn your real cost after spending credits.

Subscription credits don't roll over, and you can't get paid plan credits back. The terms say all payments are final and non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise. Reports differ on the free tier: the site says "Start for Free", but we couldn't confirm how many credits you get, and one write-up says trial credits expire after 24 hours.

## What users complain about

Pexo's Trustpilot score is 2.8 from 15 reviews, all from 2026, and nine of those are one-star. That sample is tiny, so treat it as an early warning rather than a verdict. The themes overlap:

1. **Output misses the prompt, or has poor quality: about 6.**
2. **Credits lost on failed or repeated generations: about 5.**
3. **Slow support or unanswered refund requests: about 5.**

To be fair, there are five positive reviews too, and Pexo replied to 44% of negative ones, which beats several tools we've covered.

## Things to check before paying

- **Who you're dealing with.** The terms name APEX CONTEXT PTE. LTD., while the site footer says EXTREMO LIMITED. We didn't find an explanation.
- **Your rights.** The pricing page lists commercial use rights on paid plans. The terms also give Pexo a broad, perpetual licence to your content for product development.
- **Shared links.** The terms say shareable links can't be revoked once created, so don't share anything confidential.

## Early verdict

Pexo's approach is interesting, and the workflow could save real time on short marketing videos. But unpublished credit costs, a no-refund policy and a thin, shaky review record mean we wouldn't commit much yet. If you want to try it, use one month of Pro on one real project, and track what a finished minute costs you.
