Most disappointing AI output comes from vague prompts. A short structure fixes that. We use a five-part framework we call **RCTFE**.

## The RCTFE framework

- **Role** — who the AI should act as. *"You are a senior email copywriter for B2B SaaS."*
- **Context** — what it needs to know: audience, product, goal, constraints.
- **Task** — exactly what to produce.
- **Format** — length, structure, tone, output shape (table, bullets, JSON).
- **Examples** — one good example of what you want, if you have it.

It works the same in [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt), [Claude](/tools/claude) and [Gemini](/tools/gemini).

## 10 templates you can copy

1. **Subject lines:** "Act as an email marketer. Our audience is [audience]. Write 10 subject lines under 45 characters for an email about [topic]. Mix curiosity, benefit and urgency. Return a numbered list."
2. **Welcome sequence:** "Write a 5-email welcome sequence for [product]. Goal: [goal]. Each email: subject, preview text, 120-word body, one CTA."
3. **Landing page outline:** "Create a landing page outline for [offer] aimed at [persona]. Include hero, 3 benefits, objections with answers, social proof placeholders and CTA."
4. **Ad variations:** "Write 5 Google Ads headlines (max 30 characters) and 3 descriptions (max 90 characters) for [product] targeting [keyword]."
5. **Repurposing:** "Turn this blog post into a LinkedIn post, a 5-tweet thread and a 60-second video script: [paste]."
6. **Persona research:** "Based on this customer feedback [paste], summarise the top 5 pains, desires and objections in a table."
7. **SEO brief:** "Create a content brief for the keyword [keyword]: search intent, suggested H2s, questions to answer and internal link ideas."
8. **Competitor positioning:** "Compare our product [description] with [competitor] and suggest three positioning angles we can own."
9. **Editing pass:** "Edit this copy for clarity and a [tone] tone. Keep the length. Show changes as a bulleted list after the revised text: [paste]."
10. **Review responses:** "Write a polite, specific reply to this customer review: [paste]. Acknowledge the issue and offer a next step."

## Tips that make a big difference

- **Iterate:** ask for three versions, pick the best, then refine.
- **Give constraints:** word counts and character limits reduce fluff.
- **Ask it to critique itself:** "What is weak about this draft? Fix it."
- **Never publish unchecked facts** — verify numbers and claims.
