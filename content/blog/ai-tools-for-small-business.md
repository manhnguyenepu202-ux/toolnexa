There are thousands of AI tools, but a small business only needs a handful. We picked the ones that save measurable time on work you already do.

## 1. A general AI assistant — ChatGPT or Claude

Drafting emails, summarising documents, writing job ads, brainstorming campaign ideas: a general assistant covers dozens of small tasks. [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt) is the best all-rounder; [Claude](/tools/claude) is excellent for long documents and natural writing. Not sure which? Read [ChatGPT vs Claude](/compare/chatgpt-vs-claude).

**Time saved:** 3–5 hours a week for most office roles.

## 2. Perplexity for research

When you need facts — competitor pricing, regulations, market sizes — [Perplexity](/tools/perplexity) answers with sources you can check.

## 3. Canva for marketing visuals

[Canva](/tools/canva) plus its Magic Studio AI turns a product photo into social posts, ads and presentations in minutes.

## 4. Grammarly for everything you type

[Grammarly](/tools/grammarly) catches errors and tone issues in emails, proposals and support replies — across every app.

## 5. Zapier to connect your apps

Use [Zapier](/tools/zapier) to move leads from forms into your CRM, post new blog articles to social, or summarise support emails with AI steps.

## 6. A CRM with AI built in

[HubSpot](/tools/hubspot)'s free CRM can log emails, suggest follow-ups and summarise deal history so nothing falls through the cracks.

## 7. Notion as your company brain

Put SOPs, onboarding docs and meeting notes in [Notion](/tools/notion), then ask its AI questions about your own knowledge base.

## 8. ElevenLabs for audio and video voiceovers

Need a product demo voiceover or a training video narration? [ElevenLabs](/tools/elevenlabs) produces natural voices in many languages.

## 9. An email platform with AI writing help

Most modern email tools now draft subject lines and campaigns. Pair that with a solid platform from our [best email marketing software](/best/email-marketing-software) list.

## How to roll AI out without chaos

1. **Pick one workflow** that wastes the most time and fix it first.
2. **Write down the prompt or automation** so anyone on the team can repeat it.
3. **Keep a human in the loop** for anything customer-facing.
4. **Check data policies** before pasting customer information into any tool.

Start small, measure the hours saved, and expand from there.
