Kommodo is an AI screen recorder that also turns recordings into step-by-step guides. It's priced well below Loom, and on paper it's much more generous. What's missing is independent evidence, so this is a first look, not a scored review.

We haven't used Kommodo ourselves. We read its pricing page, terms and privacy policy, plus Loom's pricing page and Kommodo's Trustpilot profile. Checked November 6, 2026.

## Why there's no score yet

Trustpilot shows a single review. Most of what's written about Kommodo online comes from its own blog or from sites promoting alternatives. That's not enough to say how reliable it is, or how good its AI guides are. We'd rather say so than guess.

## What it does

You record your screen and webcam from a Chrome extension, a desktop app or an iPhone app. You get a link to share, an AI transcript and viewer analytics. It can also record meetings through your Google calendar.

The part that sets it apart is documentation. Kommodo can turn a recording into a step-by-step guide, and it has a page builder and a knowledge base. A connected AI assistant can also read your recordings, summaries and transcripts.

For one person on yearly billing, Premium is $108 a year. For a team of five, that's $540 a year, against $1,440 for five seats of Loom Business + AI at its listed monthly price. That's a real gap, though you're also giving up Loom's long record and enterprise options.

## The catch: 60 days

This is the thing to understand before you use the free plan. Each free recording stays available to you and your viewers for 60 days. After that, you need to upgrade to get older recordings back.

So a free plan is fine for quick updates, and poor for anything people will come back to. Don't embed free recordings in your help docs or onboarding. Those links will stop working for viewers.

Premium has a similar shape. Older recordings stay accessible while your plan is active, so plan to download anything you want to keep before you cancel. Downloads themselves need Premium.

## The fine print

**Refunds are better than most.** The terms promise a full refund of your first paid subscription if you ask within 30 days, once per customer. Renewals, upgrades and extra seats aren't covered. App Store and AppSumo purchases follow those platforms' rules.

**You own your videos.** The privacy policy says so, and that they're stored until you delete them.

**Data use.** Kommodo may use data to improve its service, and it can use anonymised, aggregated data for any purpose. We found no clear statement on AI training with your recordings, so ask if that matters.

**A legal conflict.** The terms say California law, while a second block of terms on the privacy page says New York. Neither is likely to affect a small user, but it suggests the legal pages need a tidy-up.

The company is Komodo Technologies, Inc., based in New Jersey.

## Who should try it

Try Kommodo if you write SOPs or training guides and record your screen for that. Free costs nothing, and three AI guides are enough to judge the output. Check guides carefully before you share them, because we don't know how accurate they are.

Stay with Loom if your company relies on it, needs enterprise controls, or has a large library you'd have to move.
