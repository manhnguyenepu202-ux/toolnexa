## Should you wait for Black Friday?

**Wait if** you're about to buy an annual plan of a tool with a Black Friday track record, like Fliki or OpusClip. Twelve days is a short wait for a possible 50% saving.

**Don't wait if** you need the tool for work this week, or the tool rarely discounts. Start on the monthly plan or free tier now, and switch to a yearly plan if a deal appears. For Tripo, read [our Tripo review](/tools/tripo) for the real cost per model.

## How to spot a real software deal

1. **Compare against the normal yearly price**, not the monthly price. Many "50% off" banners compare a yearly deal with 12 months of monthly billing, which is usually ~20% cheaper anyway.
2. **Check what renews.** Some deals only discount the first year. Others lock in the discounted price.
3. **Read the refund terms.** Several AI tools, including Submagic and VideoGen, don't refund purchases. Our [study of 26 tools' refund policies](/blog/ai-tool-refund-policies-study) found half refuse refunds outright.
4. **Check credits, not just price.** Video and 3D tools run on credits. A cheaper plan with fewer credits may cost more per finished video or model.
5. **Screenshot the deal page and your receipt.** Billing complaints are common in this category.

## FAQ

### When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Many software sales start around November 20–24 and some run into December.

### Do AI tools have Black Friday sales?

Many do. Last year Fliki offered 50% off yearly plans, and deal sites reported 65% off OpusClip's annual Pro plan. Others, such as Tripo, reportedly didn't add an extra discount.

### Are lifetime deals worth it?

Sometimes, but be careful. A lifetime deal is only worth it if the company stays in business and keeps the features you paid for. Check what "lifetime" covers before buying.
