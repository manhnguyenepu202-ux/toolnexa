Vidu has a real following among people making anime-style AI video, and its free off-peak mode is often mentioned as a cheap way in. But pinning down what it costs and what users think turns out to be messy. So this is a first look, and we're not giving it a score yet.

## Why this is a first look, not a review

We couldn't load Vidu's pricing plans, because the page stayed on its loading message in our browser. Third-party figures disagree with each other, so we can't judge value fairly. Most reviews also describe the Q3 model, while Vidu's site now promotes Q4. We'll publish a scored review once we can confirm current prices and find evidence on the current model.

## How we evaluated this

This is research only, without first-hand test videos of our own. We read Vidu's homepage, terms, privacy policy and pricing FAQ, both of its Trustpilot profiles, and four third-party write-ups. Everything was checked on November 5, 2026.

## What Vidu is

Vidu is an AI video generator from ShengShu AI HK Limited. It offers text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video, where you supply images of characters or scenes to keep them consistent. It also lists an agent, templates and sound effects, plus an API.

Reviews agree on where it shines. Anime-style motion gets the most praise, and reference-to-video is a useful way to hold a character steady. They're less kind about realistic humans, physics and complex movement.

## Who it's for

On current evidence, Vidu suits anime creators and social video makers who want to experiment cheaply. It looks like a weaker fit for brand work that needs realistic people or strict prompt accuracy.

## Pricing: what we can and can't confirm

Vidu's pricing FAQ confirms how credits work:

- Subscription credits are valid for 30 days.
- Purchased and bonus credits are valid for 2 years.
- Monthly and annual Stripe plans renew automatically.
- It says it's unable to provide refunds.

We can't confirm plan prices. Here's what third-party sites report, and note how much they differ:

Credit costs vary as well. One write-up says about 4 credits per video, and another says about 20 for 8 seconds. If Standard's reported 800 credits is right, one site's estimate of 15 to 40 real clips after retries is a more useful planning number than the "up to 200 videos" often quoted.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot shows two separate, unclaimed profiles for the same brand: 2.2 from 18 reviews at vidu.com, and 1.8 from 27 at vidu.studio. That's a small sample, and neither shows company replies. Across the one-star reviews we read, the themes overlap:

1. **Output ignores the prompt or changes the image.** About 10 reviews, the most common complaint.
2. **Billing and credits.** Including surprise renewals, expired credits and credits charged for failed generations, in about 7.
3. **Support and account problems.** Unanswered requests and lockouts, in about 5.
4. **Moderation blocks and slow generation** come up too.

Against that, two sites report G2 at about 4.7 from 61 reviews, which we couldn't check directly. That's a sharp contrast with Trustpilot, so the true picture is unclear.

## Things to check before paying

- **Rights.** The terms limit free users to personal, non-commercial use. Paid users get commercial use.
- **Your content.** You grant Vidu a perpetual licence that includes AI training, and it survives closing your account.
- **Data.** The privacy policy says data is stored in Singapore, and processing may happen elsewhere, including China.
- **Cancelling.** Stripe subscribers are told to contact support for help. App users cancel through Apple or Google. Do it well before renewal.

## Early verdict

Vidu looks worth a free try if you make anime-style content, particularly given reports of unlimited off-peak generation. We wouldn't buy an annual plan yet. Prices are hard to verify, refunds aren't offered, and users raise real concerns. Test it on your own prompts, then go monthly if the results hold up.

## FAQ

### Is Vidu free?

Vidu says users receive free credits, and third parties report unlimited off-peak generation. We couldn't confirm the current amounts.

### How much does Vidu cost?

Third parties report plans starting around $8 to $12 a month. We couldn't confirm this on Vidu's own page.

### Can I get a refund from Vidu?

Its FAQ says it's unable to provide refunds, and its terms say payments are final unless the law requires otherwise.

### Can I use Vidu videos commercially?

Paid users can, according to its terms. Free use is personal and non-commercial.
