Toolsaday is a bundle of AI writing tools, including an email generator, a paraphraser, a story generator and a plagiarism checker. The email generator is the page most people land on, and it's billed as free. The paid plans start at $7.99 a month.

We haven't used Toolsaday ourselves. We read its email generator page, pricing page, terms and privacy policy, its Trustpilot and SourceForge reviews, and a third-party listing. Checked November 9, 2026.

## Why there's no score yet

There isn't enough independent evidence. Trustpilot shows 7 reviews, and two of those seem to be about a different product entirely, a pair of glasses. SourceForge has 4. Most other write-ups we found are directory listings with conflicting prices, some of them clearly out of date. So this is a first look, not a scored review.

## What the email generator does

You choose whether to write a new email or reply to one. Then you describe the purpose, and optionally add a subject line, recipient and sender. You can set a length from 50 to 500 words, a tone, a language, and plain text or HTML output. For replies, you paste in the email you received and say what you want your answer to achieve.

That's a sensible set of controls. It's also roughly what you'd get by typing the same details into a general chatbot. The page doesn't say how many free emails you get or whether you need an account, and we couldn't confirm either without signing up.

Standard includes 1 million characters of text generation a month. A 200-word email is roughly 1,200 characters, so that's in the region of 800 emails, by our rough maths. For email alone, Standard is more than enough.

Pro and above add unlimited use of Toolsaday's basic models and custom AI tools. The models have house names like Cheetah and Jaguar, and the site doesn't say which AI companies are behind them.

There's no free trial, the FAQ says, but there is a free version. Its limits aren't published on the pricing page.

## The fine print

**Old legal pages.** The terms and privacy policy both say they were last updated on March 11, 2021. They don't name a registered company or an address. The governing law clause names the United States but leaves the state blank, which looks like unfinished template text. For a company that takes card payments, that's a weak start.

**A three-day refund window.** You can ask for a refund for dissatisfaction within 3 days of purchase. Billing errors and long outages are covered separately. Cancellation after a renewal date isn't refundable, and cancelling stops the next charge but keeps access until the period ends.

**Training on your data.** The pricing FAQ says customer data isn't used to train its AI. We didn't find that promise in the privacy policy itself.

**Commercial use.** The FAQ says you can use outputs commercially.

## What users report

The evidence is thin, but it points the same way. Excluding the two reviews that seem to be about glasses, the recent complaints are:

- **Billing and renewals.** One reviewer says a renewal was charged after they thought they had cancelled, and that emails went unanswered. The same person, on SourceForge, says the company didn't follow its own refund policy.
- **Output quality.** Reports of repeated words, and of a story tool that ignores direction.
- **No save function.** One reviewer couldn't save work.

Some reviewers like it for getting past writer's block and for formal letters. SourceForge's support sub-score is 3.0, the lowest of its four.

## Who should try it

Try the free email generator if you want a quick draft and you'd rather fill in a form than write a prompt. That costs nothing.

Be careful about paying. If you do, take the monthly Standard plan first, not a yearly one, and diary the renewal date. The 3-day refund window and the billing complaints mean you shouldn't count on getting money back.

If you already pay for [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt), it will write the same emails, and it has a much longer public record. [Grammarly](/tools/grammarly) is the better pick if you mostly want to fix emails you've written yourself.
