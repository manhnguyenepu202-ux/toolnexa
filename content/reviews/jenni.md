## What Jenni AI actually is

Jenni is a document editor that writes alongside you. As you type, it suggests the next sentence. You can upload PDFs, chat with them, and pull citations into your text in thousands of styles.

The key thing is the workflow. Used well, you're still the author, and Jenni speeds up the slow bits. Used badly, it writes confident paragraphs you haven't thought through.

## Who it's for

- **The postgraduate.** You've read your sources and want help getting them into words.
- **The researcher.** You're handling a long paper with lots of references.
- **The non-native English writer.** You know what you want to say, and want help phrasing it.

It's a poor fit for anyone hoping to avoid the reading. It's also a poor fit for proofreading a finished paper, which is better done by a tool made for polishing.

## Where it's strong

**Citations come built in.** Even the free plan includes unlimited citations, and Jenni advertises 10,000+ styles. Reviewers do note other pages quote a smaller number, but any normal style should be covered.

**A real free plan.** You get 10 autocompletes a day, along with a small number of edits and chats. It's enough to see whether the writing style suits you.

**Your own library.** Uploading PDFs keeps suggestions tied to your sources, which is safer than a general chatbot.

**A clear no-training statement.** Jenni's teams page says it doesn't use your text, prompts, citations or files to train language models. That's a marketing page rather than the legal terms, but it's a clear claim.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot shows 3.7 from 161 reviews, with 64% five-star and 22% one-star. Only 36 came in the last 12 months, and Trustpilot says Jenni hasn't replied to negative reviews.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated March 2025 to October 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Billing or unexpected charges: 12.**
2. **Cancelling: 10.** Including charges after cancelling and a broken cancel button.
3. **Refunds: 8.**
4. **Support: 8.** No reply, or slow replies.
5. **Output quality: 6.**
6. **Technical problems: 3.**

None of the 20 complained specifically about fake citations, which is mildly reassuring. But it isn't proof that it doesn't happen, and we found no solid data either way. AI tools can produce references that look real and aren't, so open every source yourself.

**One 2026 reviewer reports a $144 charge after cancelling.** That's the price of a year of Plus, and it shows what's at stake. Cancel well before your renewal date, and keep the confirmation.

**Your drafts and analytics.** This one surprised us. The privacy policy says some of what you write goes to PostHog, its analytics provider. Session replay can capture the text of your document, including unpublished drafts, and it runs by default unless you deny analytics cookies in settings. If your paper has unpublished data, turn that off first.

**Who owns the output?** We didn't find a clause on this in the terms, which were last updated in April 2024. That doesn't mean Jenni claims your work, but it's an odd gap for a writing tool.

**Disputes.** The terms send most disputes to binding arbitration in Delaware, with a class-action waiver.

## Academic integrity

No tool can make this decision for you. Check your university or journal's rules first. ICMJE, whose recommendations many medical journals follow, asks authors to disclose AI use, says AI can't be an author, and holds humans responsible for the result.

Jenni has no built-in AI detector, according to reviewers, so it can't tell you how your text will score elsewhere. And detectors shouldn't be the point. The safe approach is to be able to explain and defend every sentence.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly, per month | Yearly total | Autocompletes | AI edits | AI chat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | $0 | 10 a day | 3 | 5 |
| Plus | $30 | $12 | $144 | 5,000 a month | 500 a month | 500 a month |
| Pro | $73 | $29 | $348 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

The prices you'll see quoted first, $12 and $29, are yearly rates. Pay monthly and it's $30 and $73, so the 60% discount is large, but only if you stay.

Here's the maths. Yearly Plus equals 4.8 months of monthly billing, and so does yearly Pro. So paying yearly only makes sense if you'll use it for five months or more.

| Your situation | Best plan | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| One essay this term | Free, then Plus for a month | Up to $30 |
| A three-month dissertation push | Plus monthly | $90 |
| A long thesis or regular papers | Plus yearly | $144 |
| Writing all day, every day | Pro yearly | $348 |

Plus has plenty for most people. 500 AI edits a month is about 25 a working day. Free downloads have one more limit: export covers body content only, so you'll need a paid plan for a full document export.

Payments are generally non-refundable, except where the law says otherwise. EU users may have a 14-day right. Jenni says refunds for bugs need a call with support and a demonstration.

**Which plan should you pick?** Try Free for a week. Then pay monthly for Plus, and only switch to yearly if you're sure you'll use it for five months or more. Set a calendar reminder for the renewal date, either way.

## Your first hour

1. Check your institution's AI rules.
2. In settings, deny analytics cookies before you upload anything sensitive.
3. Upload three PDFs you've actually read.
4. Draft one section, then check each citation against the source.
5. Look at Billing, so you know where to cancel.

## Look elsewhere when

- Your institution doesn't allow AI drafting.
- You're working with confidential or unpublished data and can't accept analytics risk.
- You only need proofreading.
- You want dependable billing support.

## Verdict

Jenni is a good drafting partner for people who've done the reading. Citations and PDF chat save real time, and Plus at $144 a year is reasonable for a long project. The risks are around the product, not in it. Renewal and cancellation complaints are common, and the default analytics setting is too broad for research drafts. So change that setting first, keep your cancellation proof, and stay in charge of every sentence.

## FAQ

### Is Jenni AI free?

Yes. The free plan gives 10 autocompletes a day, unlimited citations, and a small allowance of edits and chats.

### Is Jenni AI plagiarism?

Not by itself, but how you use it matters. Follow your institution's rules, and disclose AI use where it's required.

### Are Jenni's citations reliable?

Don't assume so. Check each reference against the original source.

### Does Jenni AI train on my work?

Its teams page says it doesn't. Its privacy policy does say document content can reach its analytics provider, unless you deny analytics cookies.

### Can I get a refund from Jenni AI?

Generally no. EU users may have a 14-day right, and bug refunds need a support call.
