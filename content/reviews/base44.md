## What Base44 actually is

You describe an app in plain language, and Base44 builds the front end, database, login, hosting and common integrations. It's owned by Wix, which is a reassuring name to have behind a young product.

The draw is that everything comes bundled. You don't connect a separate database or hosting account. The trade-off is that your app lives inside Base44's world, and that's the question to think about before you commit.

## Who it's for

- **The non-technical founder.** You want a working prototype this week, and you don't want to learn Supabase.
- **The small business owner.** A booking tool or simple internal app is a sensible fit.
- **The product manager.** You want something clickable to show people, not production software.

It's a poor fit for developers who want control of the backend, or for anyone expecting complex software to come out of a few prompts.

## Where it's strong

**Very low setup effort.** Reviewers regularly say its build commentary is easier for non-coders to follow than rivals'.

**A clear credit guide.** The docs say what kinds of action cost what, from about 0.5 credits for small changes to 3 or 4 for big ones. Manual visual edits are free, and so are automatic AI fixes on paid plans.

**You own what's generated.** The terms say customers own the code and apps the platform generates.

**It answers public complaints.** Trustpilot shows Base44 replying to 59% of negative reviews. Whether those replies help is another matter.

## What users complain about

Base44's Trustpilot score is 2.7 from 927 reviews, with 33% five-star and 54% one-star. Those are recent too, with 748 from the last 12 months. Product Hunt tells a kinder story, at about 4.4 to 4.5 from around 30 reviews, but that's a small and often early-adopter crowd.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated from August to November 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Support: about 16.** Slow, unhelpful or AI-only help.
2. **Billing and refunds: 13.** Includes four people who say they bought an annual plan by accident.
3. **Cancelling or being locked in: about 7.**
4. **Upsells by third parties: about 5.** Some reviewers blame resellers or affiliates for pushing them, which is worth knowing since we're an affiliate ourselves.
5. **AI output quality: about 5.**
6. **Credits wasted on AI errors: 4.**

The accidental annual plans have a likely cause: yearly is the default tab on the pricing page. So check the total at checkout, and don't assume you're paying monthly.

**Lock-in is disputed.** Base44's FAQ says two-way GitHub sync exports your full source code and nothing is locked. Several reviewers say the backend and database stay on Base44, with no clean way out. We couldn't test this, so check it yourself on real data before relying on it.

**Your content.** The terms give Base44 a licence that includes training its software tools, and we found no opt-out. One review also mentions a past security issue, and another a multi-day outage in February 2026. We couldn't verify either, so treat them as questions to ask.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Yearly billing | Implied monthly | Message credits | Integration credits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 25 a month | 100 |
| Starter | $16/mo | About $20 | 100 | 2,000 |
| Builder | $40/mo | About $50 | 250 | 10,000 |
| Pro | $80/mo | About $100 | 500 | 20,000 |
| Elite | $160/mo | About $200 | 1,200 | 50,000 |

Monthly figures are our maths from the 20% yearly saving, so confirm them at checkout.

Here's the catch. Starter doesn't include backend functions or GitHub. Both start on Builder, so the true entry price for a serious app is $40 a month, or $480 up front for the year.

What do 100 message credits buy? Using Base44's ranges, here's one month of building:

| Work | Credits each | Count | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large changes | 4 | 5 | 20 |
| Complex modules | 2 | 20 | 40 |
| Small features | 1 | 30 | 30 |
| Quick tweaks | 0.5 | 20 | 10 |
| **Total** | | | **100** |

That's fine for a simple app, but tight if the AI goes wrong. Automatic fixes are free on paid plans, but your own repeat prompts aren't, and the docs say credits aren't refundable for AI mistakes.

Integration credits cover what your live app does. An email costs about 1, and an in-app AI agent starts at about 3 per message, so Builder's 10,000 credits cover about 3,300 agent replies.

Unused credits expire at reset, so you can't save them up. Fees are non-refundable, and if integration credits run out, those actions fail.

**Which plan should you pick?** Use Free to test your idea, and move to Builder on monthly billing if you need a backend. Only go yearly once the app has survived a full month.

## Your first hour

1. Build the smallest version of your idea on Free.
2. Note how many credits one real feature uses.
3. Try exporting on a paid plan before you depend on it, including the data.
4. Add a real email or AI feature, and watch your integration credits.
5. At checkout, switch to Monthly and check the total before paying.

## Look elsewhere when

- Owning your stack matters. Compare Lovable first.
- You're building something complex or regulated. Talk to a developer.
- You need dependable human support.
- You'd be uncomfortable with your prompts being used for training.

## Verdict

Base44 is one of the easiest ways to go from idea to working app, and Wix's backing helps. But more than half of its Trustpilot reviews are one-star, and the complaints are about money and support, not just bugs. Use it to test an idea cheaply and on monthly billing. Before a real business depends on it, prove you can get your app and your data out.

## FAQ

### Is Base44 free?

Yes. The free plan gives up to 25 message credits a month, capped at 5 a day, and up to 5 apps.

### Does Base44 have monthly billing?

Yes, though yearly is shown first. Check the toggle and your total at checkout.

### Can I export my code?

Base44 says GitHub sync on Builder and above exports your full source. Reviewers report that the backend and database are harder to move, so test it.

### Does Base44 refund credits for AI mistakes?

No. The docs say credits aren't refundable for tool behaviour or AI mistakes.

### Who owns Base44?

Wix.com Ltd.
