## What Crayo actually is

Crayo is built around the kind of short videos you've seen on TikTok and Reels. You paste or generate a Reddit-style story, add background gameplay and an AI voice, and it builds a vertical video. It also makes fake text-message videos, and AutoClip turns long videos into shorter clips.

It's a production tool, not a growth system. Third-party reviews say you still download and post videos yourself. There's also a newer iPhone app, released in October 2026.

## Who it's for

- **The hobby creator.** You like these formats and want to skip the editing work.
- **The clipper.** You cut long videos into shorts, and have the right to use them.
- **The side-project tester.** You want to try a faceless channel for a month or two.

It's a poor fit for brands, because the licence wording is unclear, as you'll see below. It's also a poor fit for anyone sold on the idea of passive income from automated channels. Nothing here makes that happen.

## Where it's strong

**It's quick.** The format templates remove most of the editing work, and that's why people use it.

**One credit, one video.** Crayo defines a workflow credit as one generated video, which is simple to understand.

**Cancelling is self-service.** The policy says anyone can cancel in Settings, with no approval needed. Only 2 of the 20 latest one-star reviews were about being unable to cancel or being charged afterwards.

**It answers complaints.** Trustpilot shows Crayo replying to 83% of negative reviews, usually within a week. Many of those replies deny the claim or send people to email, though.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot shows 2.7 from 198 reviews, split sharply: 48% five-star and 43% one-star. There are 64 reviews from the last 12 months, so it's a live picture. The new iPhone app has 4.4 from only 14 ratings, which is too early to mean much.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated July 8 to November 4, 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Billing or unexpected charges: about 12.**
2. **Support: about 10.** No response or a runaround.
3. **Refunds: about 9.**
4. **Technical problems: about 6.** Editor errors, login trouble and downloads.
5. **Output quality: about 4.** Stretched video, missing sound and ignored clip lengths.

The most striking pattern is that about 8 of the 20 mention a $5 "Starter Kit", an AI clipping course, or a required subscription sold by a third party. Crayo replies that it has no $5 course, and no tie to Base44, which some of those reviewers also mention. We can't say who's behind those offers. But if a course or funnel tells you that you must buy Crayo, stop and read the checkout carefully. All sales are final.

**The commercial licence conflict is real.** The pricing FAQ says you fully own the videos you create. The Terms of Service, last updated in March 2024, grant access for "personal, non-commercial purposes". Those two can't both be the whole story. The terms were written earlier and may simply be out of date, but if you're running a business, email support@crayo.ai and get a written answer first.

**YouTube's rules matter more than any feature.** YouTube says monetized content can't be mass-produced or repetitive, and it specifically names AI content made with generic templates. Automation is allowed, but your video has to show creative input. A channel of near-identical Reddit videos is exactly what that policy describes, so don't budget on ad revenue.

**Rights.** Crayo doesn't give you rights to what you clip. If you're cutting someone else's stream or show, you need permission or a clipping programme that allows it.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Every 3 months | Yearly | Credits | Export | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hobby | $29 | $57 | $199 | 250 | 2 hours | 30 min |
| Clipper | $59 | $117 | $399 | 1,000 | 10 hours | 120 min |
| Pro | $129 | $237 | $799 | 3,000 | 30 hours | 180 min |

The $19 headline price is the quarterly rate, billed as $57 up front. Yearly billing saves $149 to $749, but it means paying up to $799 with no way back.

Here's the catch. You have four separate meters, and the smallest one sets your real limit. Say you make one-minute Reddit videos with a voiceover on Hobby:

| Limit | Allowance | One-minute videos |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow credits | 250 | 250 |
| Export | 120 minutes | 120 |
| Voiceover | 30 minutes | 30 |

So 250 credits really means about 30 narrated videos, or one a day. That's about 97 cents a video on monthly billing. Clipper's 120 voiceover minutes bring that to about 49 cents, which makes it the better deal. Pro adds only 60 more voiceover minutes, so it's poor value for narration and only makes sense for clipping at volume.

We can't say how many credits an AutoClip upload uses if it produces many clips, so check that in the app. Unused allowances don't roll over.

The App Store lists different in-app prices, such as Clipper at $39 a month and $324.99 a year. Crayo's site says prices are the same, but they don't appear to be, so compare both before you choose. Apple's own refund process may also apply to App Store purchases, which isn't the case on the web.

**Which plan should you pick?** Buy one month of Hobby, and make ten videos. Never start on yearly. If you post daily with voiceovers, move to Clipper.

## Verdict

Crayo does a narrow job fast. If you enjoy Reddit-story and fake-text formats, one month costs $29, and you'll know quickly whether it's for you. But we can't recommend it past that without caveats. All sales are final even for accidental purchases, its legal terms conflict on commercial use, and the recent complaints show real confusion about what people are buying. Pay monthly, treat it as a tool, and ignore anyone promising income.

## FAQ

### Does Crayo have a free plan?

No. We found no free plan or trial on the pricing page. Crayo does offer a few separate free utility tools.

### Does Crayo give refunds?

No. The policy says all sales are final, and it names accidental purchases as an example.

### Can I use Crayo videos commercially?

The FAQ says you own your videos, while the terms mention personal, non-commercial use. Get a written answer from support if it matters.

### Will Crayo videos get monetized on YouTube?

Not automatically. YouTube's rules exclude mass-produced, template-based content, so your videos need clear creative input.

### Is the $5 Crayo starter kit real?

Crayo says it has no $5 course. Be careful with any third-party offer that requires a Crayo subscription.
