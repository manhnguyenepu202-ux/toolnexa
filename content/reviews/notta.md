## What Notta actually is

Notta turns speech into text in three ways: a bot that joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls; uploads of audio and video files; and a mobile app that records in-person conversations. After transcription it writes an AI summary, picks out action items, and can translate the transcript.

Its standout is language coverage. Notta transcribes 58 languages and handles bilingual conversations, which most English-first note-takers don't do well.

## Who it's for

- **Multilingual teams** with meetings that switch between languages.
- **Students and researchers** transcribing interviews and lectures.
- **Freelancers and podcasters** who need cheap file transcription.
- **Anyone recording in person** with a phone.

## Where it's strong

**Languages.** 58 transcription languages plus translation is broad for this price.

**Price on the yearly plan.** Around $98 a year for 1,800 minutes a month is cheaper than most well-known rivals.

**Several ways in.** Live meetings, uploads and phone recordings all end up in one searchable library.

**Ratings outside Trustpilot.** Notta averages about 4.4 on G2 and about 4.1 on the App Store, where users mostly praise speed and accuracy on clear audio.

## What users complain about

- **Charges after cancelling.** This is the big one. Notta's Trustpilot page currently shows no overall score, but around 170 reviews, most of them one-star, with many describing renewals or charges after the user believed they had cancelled.
- **Hard-to-reach support** when trying to get refunds.
- **Accuracy with accents and crosstalk.** Clear audio transcribes well; overlapping speakers and strong accents need more editing.
- **The free plan is really a demo.** A 3-minute cap per recording rules out real meetings.
- **The meeting bot.** Some participants and companies don't allow note-taking bots.

## How to protect yourself before paying

1. Pay with a card that lets you set spending limits, or a virtual card.
2. Screenshot the cancellation confirmation and keep the email.
3. Turn off auto-renew in your account the day you subscribe if you only want one term.
4. Check your statement after the renewal date.

## Verdict

Notta does a lot for not much money, especially if you work in several languages or transcribe long recordings. The billing complaints are too common to ignore, though. If you choose it, pay yearly only once you've tested Pro for a month, use a card you control, and keep proof of any cancellation.

## FAQ

### Is Notta free?

### How many languages does Notta support?

58 for transcription, with translation on paid plans.

### Does Notta join meetings as a bot?

Yes, for live Zoom, Meet and Teams calls. You can also upload recordings instead.
