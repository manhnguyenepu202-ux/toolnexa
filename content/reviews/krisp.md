## What Krisp actually is

Krisp sits between your microphone and your call app. It filters out keyboards, dogs, traffic and other people talking near you, in both directions, so you sound clean and so do the people you hear. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles and most other apps that let you pick a microphone.

On top of that, Krisp records and transcribes the meeting from your own device, then writes AI notes and action items. The difference from tools like Otter or Fireflies is that no bot joins the call, so other participants don't see an extra "note-taker" in the list.

## Who it's for

- **The home worker with a noisy household.** Kids, pets and building work are exactly what it's built to remove.
- **Sales and support teams** taking back-to-back calls in open offices.
- **People who dislike meeting bots** or work with clients who refuse them.
- **Non-native English speakers** who want to try Krisp's accent conversion feature.

## Where it's strong

**Noise cancellation.** This is what Krisp is known for, and it's the reason it holds a rating around 4.6–4.7 out of 5 on G2 from well over a thousand reviews. Users repeatedly mention that background voices, not just fans and typing, disappear.

**No meeting bot.** Notes come from audio captured on your computer. That's quieter socially, and you don't need host permission to add a bot.

**Works almost everywhere.** Because it acts as a virtual microphone and speaker, any call app that lets you choose devices can use it.

**A useful free plan.** Sixty minutes a day of noise cancellation covers many people's daily calls, and transcription is included.

## What users complain about

- **Support and billing.** Trustpilot reviews are noticeably more negative than G2, with complaints about slow support replies and problems around cancellations.
- **Lost or missing notes.** Some users report recordings or notes that didn't save. Keep important meetings backed up another way until you trust it.
- **Short free history.** Notes on the free plan are kept for 7 days, so it's a trial for note-taking rather than an archive.
- **Desktop focus.** The noise cancellation people pay for is built around the desktop app, not phone calls.
- **Resource use.** On older laptops, a few users notice extra CPU load during calls.

## Krisp vs rivals

**Notta** is cheaper for heavy transcription and covers far more languages, but it sends a bot into meetings and has serious billing complaints. See our [Krisp vs Notta comparison](/compare/krisp-vs-notta).

**Built-in noise suppression** in Zoom, Teams and Meet is free and good enough for many people. Try it first if your background is only mildly noisy.

**NVIDIA Broadcast** is a free alternative for noise removal if you have a supported NVIDIA graphics card, though it has no meeting notes.

## Your first hour

1. Install the desktop app and set Krisp as the microphone and speaker in your call app.
2. Join a test call with a fan or music on and listen to a recording of yourself.
3. Check how many minutes a day you actually use before paying.
4. Run one real meeting with notes on, and read the summary against your own memory.
5. If you upgrade, put a calendar reminder a week before renewal.

## Look elsewhere when

- You need transcription in many languages.
- You take most calls on a phone.
- You want a free, long-term archive of meeting notes.
- Your call app's built-in noise suppression already does the job.

## Verdict

Krisp is the easiest way to sound professional from a noisy place, and the bot-free notes are a genuine extra rather than a gimmick. Start on the free plan, because 60 minutes a day tells you quickly whether you need more. If you pay, the yearly plan is the sensible choice, but set a renewal reminder given the support complaints.

## FAQ

### Is Krisp free?

Yes. The free plan includes 60 minutes of noise cancellation a day, transcription and a small number of AI notes, with 7 days of history.

### Does Krisp join my meetings as a bot?

No. It records and transcribes from your own device, so no extra participant appears.

### Does Krisp work with Zoom and Teams?

Yes. It works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and most apps that let you choose a microphone and speaker.

### Does Krisp work on my phone?

Its noise cancellation is mainly a desktop feature. Check the current app store listing for what the mobile version covers.
