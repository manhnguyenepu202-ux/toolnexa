## Key takeaways

- **What it is:** an all-in-one social media platform for publishing, inbox, reporting, reviews and listening.
- **What it really costs:** $99, $199 or $449 a month, or about 20% less on yearly billing. X access adds $29 a month.
- **Best for:** agencies and multi-location businesses that manage many profiles with a few people.
- **Skip it if:** you run a handful of channels alone, or you need phone support.
- **Our score:** 8.0/10. Strong value at scale, marked down by a serious question over its reviews.

## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based review, without first-hand use of our own. We read Vista Social's pricing page, FAQ, terms and privacy policy, its Trustpilot profile and all 13 visible one-star reviews, plus third-party reviews. Its help centre blocked our reader, and G2 figures came through other sites. Everything was checked on November 5, 2026.

## The issue to know first

Trustpilot doesn't show a score for Vista Social. Its page says the rating is unavailable because of a breach of its guidelines, and that it removed a number of fake reviews for this company.

Trustpilot's notice doesn't say who wrote them, so we can't draw conclusions about that. It does mean we can't lean on the review counts you may see quoted, and we'd treat glowing public praise with more care than usual. Other sites look strong, with about 4.8 reported on G2 and 4.9 from 968 reviews on Software Advice. We couldn't check how those reviews were collected.

## What Vista Social actually is

Vista Social pulls social media work into one place. You schedule posts across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Reddit and more. There's a shared inbox for comments and messages, review management, reports you can send to clients, a link-in-bio page and AI writing help.

The big difference is how it charges. Buffer bills per channel, and Hootsuite bills per user. Vista Social sells flat plans with a set number of profiles and users, and that shapes who it suits.

## Who it's for

- **The small agency.** Two or three people with 15 to 30 client profiles get the most from flat pricing.
- **The multi-location business.** Review management across Google, Yelp and TripAdvisor is useful if you run many sites.
- **The growing brand team.** It's a fit if you need approvals, an inbox and reports in one tool.

It's a poor fit for a solo creator with three accounts. You'd pay $99 for capacity you won't use.

## Where it's strong

**Price per profile.** Every plan works out at about $6.50 a profile each month. Rival per-seat tools get expensive fast as your team grows.

**Lots included.** Unlimited scheduled posts, reporting, an inbox and a link-in-bio page come with the base plan, and own-profile listening is free.

**Wide network support.** Reviewers often mention that it reaches more networks than rivals, and that it covers review sites too.

**Ease of use.** It's the main praise on third-party sites, though that's the same data we've asked you to read with care.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot still shows 78 reviews, 79% five-star and 17% one-star, with 26 from the last 12 months. We read all 13 visible one-star reviews, six of them from the past year. Themes overlap:

1. **Billing and price rises: 5.** Includes rising prices and trouble cancelling.
2. **Support: 5.** Slow or absent replies, plus a missed onboarding call.
3. **Accounts cancelled or suspended: 4.** One reviewer says this followed a negative review posted elsewhere. We can't verify that.
4. **Bugs: 4.** Comments disappearing and duplicate posts.
5. **Lifetime deal changes: 3.** AppSumo buyers say features such as X access were removed.

We saw no company replies to any of the 13. Third-party reviewers add that the mobile app is weaker than the desktop product, and that heavy users can run through AI credits.

The lifetime deal reports matter even if you're paying monthly, since they show how terms can shift. Vista Social's terms also let annual renewals rise by up to 7%, or more with 60 days' notice.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly, per month | Profiles | Users | Per profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | $99 | About $79 | 15 | 2 | $6.60 |
| Advanced | $199 | About $159 | 30 | 3 | $6.63 |
| Scale | $449 | About $359 | 70 | 4 | $6.41 |

Those per-profile figures only look good if you fill the plan. Here's what real setups cost:

| Who | Setup | Cost per month |
|---|---|---|
| Local business, 6 profiles, no X | Professional | $99, or $16.50 per profile used |
| Small agency, 15 profiles, X | Professional + X | $128 |
| Agency, 30 profiles, X, listening | Advanced + X + listening | $303 |

Four things to know:

- **X costs extra on every plan.** That's $29 a month.
- **Full listening costs extra.** Tracking your own profiles is free, but web and news listening is $75 a month.
- **White label needs Scale.** If agency branding matters, your entry price is $449.
- **Extra users aren't priced publicly.** Users stop at two on Professional, so ask before your team grows.

**Which plan should you pick?** Professional on monthly billing, during and after the 14-day trial. Fees are non-refundable, and annual plans renew unless you cancel at least 30 days ahead, so diary that date if you switch.

## Vista Social vs rivals

**Buffer** is simpler and cheaper for small setups. It has a free plan and per-channel pricing. Pick Buffer if you run a few channels. See our [Vista Social vs Buffer comparison](/compare/vista-social-vs-buffer).

**Hootsuite** lists $99 per user for 10 accounts on its Standard plan. Pick Hootsuite if you want a long-established name and have a small team.

## Your first hour

1. Start the 14-day trial, which needs no card.
2. Connect your real profiles and check that each network supports what you post.
3. Schedule a week of content and check it on your phone.
4. Build one client report and see if you'd really send it.
5. Ask support what an extra user costs, and note how quickly they reply.

## Look elsewhere when

- You manage under about 10 channels. Buffer will cost less.
- You need phone or live chat support. Vista Social offers neither.
- You need white label below $449 a month.
- Vendor trust is a formal part of your buying process, and Trustpilot's notice worries you.

## Verdict

On features and price, Vista Social makes a strong case for small agencies, and we'd shortlist it. Fill your plan, count X and listening, and it beats per-seat tools comfortably. But we can't ignore a platform notice about fake reviews, and we'd want to see how support treats you before paying for a year. Use the free trial properly, go monthly, and decide on what you see yourself rather than on anyone's star rating.

## FAQ

### Is Vista Social free?

No. There's a 14-day trial with no card needed, then plans start at $99 a month.

### Why doesn't Vista Social have a Trustpilot score?

Trustpilot says the rating is unavailable because of a guideline breach, and that it removed a number of fake reviews for the company.

### Does Vista Social charge extra for X?

Yes, X access is a $29 monthly add-on on every plan.

### Can I get a refund?

The terms say fees are non-refundable, and cancelling keeps access to the end of the billing period.

### Is Vista Social good for agencies?

On price and features, yes, especially with 15 or more profiles and a small team. White label needs the $449 Scale plan.
