## What EaseUS Data Recovery Wizard actually is

You install it, pick a drive, and it scans for files that have been deleted or lost. It shows what it found, you preview them, and you restore the ones you want. EaseUS has been around since 2004, and this is its best-known product.

One tip before anything else: install it on a different drive from the one you're rescuing, and restore files to a different drive too. Writing to the damaged disk can overwrite the very files you want back. If that's where you are right now, stop using that drive.

## Who it's for

- **The accidental deleter.** You emptied the Recycle Bin, or wiped the wrong folder on a hard drive.
- **The person with a formatted drive.** A USB stick or hard disk was formatted, but it still works normally.
- **The small IT helper.** The Technician edition covers several PCs and servers, which suits a repair shop.

It's the wrong tool for a drive with a physical fault. If the disk clicks, grinds or doesn't show up, every scan puts it at more risk. Talk to a recovery lab first, especially if the data matters.

## Where it's strong

**It's genuinely easy.** This is the praise that comes up most often, and TechRadar agrees. You don't have to understand file systems to get a result.

**Good results on ordinary drives.** Digital Trends ran hands-on tests in 2024. EaseUS recovered hard-drive files and folders in every test, and restored a damaged thumb drive.

**A useful free version.** EaseUS advertises up to 2 GB of free recovery, though a review quoted on the same page mentions 1 GB, so check what you get. Either way, you can see whether your files are found before you pay. That's the most useful thing about it.

**Clear one-off pricing.** The pricing page says "no renewal fees" on all three plans. For a tool most people need once, that's what you want.

## What users complain about

Capterra shows 4.6 out of 5 from about 411 reviews, and Software Advice shows 4.6 from about 413. Trustpilot is harder to read. Search listings show 4.8 from 753 reviews on one page and 4.0 from 463 on another, while Digital Trends counted 23,000 reviews at 4.7 back in 2024. We couldn't open the profile, so we wouldn't lean on any of those numbers.

The complaint themes are clearer, though we can't rank them by how often they appear:

1. **Refunds.** This is the most repeated grievance. Reviewers describe being refused despite the 30-day guarantee, or being offered partial money or more free time instead.
2. **Support as a gate.** Some say they must go through technical support before a refund is considered, and the policy confirms it.
3. **Failed or corrupt recoveries.** Software Advice's summary notes failures in complex cases. Rossmann Group, a repair lab with its own business interest, says failed recoveries can be refused refunds as hardware damage.
4. **Cost and the free limit.** GetApp's summary flags both.

There's also an older complaint worth checking. A 2019 forum user said the one-month plan was an auto-renewing subscription at a higher price. Today's page says there are no renewal fees, and we found nothing that says otherwise, but look for a renewal line at checkout anyway.

PissedConsumer shows 1.9 from 131 reviews. Complaint sites attract unhappy customers, so read that as a list of what can go wrong, not a fair average.

**SSDs are the big limit.** Digital Trends found EaseUS was the only tool that restored files from a quick-formatted SSD, and the writer suspected luck. It couldn't get back files simply deleted from the SSD. Most modern laptops use SSDs, so if that's you, lower your hopes.

**The 99.7% claim.** EaseUS advertises a 99.7% success rate. We found no independent test that backs it up, and TechRadar reported it missing files that lesser tools found in one test. Treat it as marketing.

Two separate one-month purchases come to $139.90, and three come to $209.85. So Lifetime only pays off at your third incident, or if you look after other people's computers.

**Check the refund terms before paying.** The 30-day guarantee is narrower than it sounds. Refunds apply only on technical grounds, you must let support try first, and a product that has "been used successfully" doesn't qualify. If a recovery partly works, expect a discussion. You also buy under Chinese law, as EaseUS is run by Chengdu Yiwo Tech Development, so a dispute wouldn't be simple for most buyers.

**Which plan should you pick?** Run the free scan first. If your files show up in the preview, buy One-Month, recover them, and stop there. Most people don't need anything more.

## Your first hour

1. Stop writing to the affected drive.
2. Install the free version on a different drive.
3. Run a scan, then preview the files you care about.
4. Open a few previews fully, since a filename alone proves little.
5. Only then pay, and restore everything to a different drive.

## Look elsewhere when

- The drive clicks, overheats or isn't detected. Go to a lab.
- You've deleted files from an SSD and kept using it. Chances are poor.
- You need a guaranteed refund. This policy doesn't offer one.
- You want a recovery tool for occasional use on Mac at a low price. Compare Disk Drill first.

## Verdict

EaseUS Data Recovery Wizard is a sensible first move when you've deleted files from a working hard drive or USB stick. It's simple, and the free preview shows you what's possible before you spend anything. Just buy the month, not the lifetime plan, unless you have a real reason. And don't let the 99.7% figure talk you out of calling a lab when a drive is physically failing.

## FAQ

### Is EaseUS Data Recovery Wizard free?

There's a free version, advertised at up to 2 GB of recovered data. Larger recoveries need a paid plan.

### Does EaseUS have a monthly subscription?

The current page sells a one-month licence with "no renewal fees". An older user complaint described auto-renewal, so check your checkout page.

### Can EaseUS recover files from an SSD?

Sometimes, but don't count on it. In Digital Trends' test, it restored files after a quick format once, and couldn't recover deleted ones.

### Will EaseUS refund me if it doesn't work?

Possibly. The 30-day guarantee covers technical failures that support can't solve. It excludes non-technical reasons and products already used successfully.

### Is EaseUS safe to use?

EaseUS has sold this software since 2004. We didn't audit it ourselves, so download only from the official site, and avoid cracked versions and reseller keys.
