## What Fliki actually is

You give Fliki an idea, a script, a blog post or a slide deck. It writes or splits the text into scenes, adds an AI voice, matches stock clips or images, and hands you a video you can edit like a document. You can also skip the video and make audio only.

The voices are the main draw. Fliki advertises 80+ languages, and Standard includes 1,000 voices, 500 of them labelled ultra-realistic. Think of it as a voiceover tool that also makes videos, rather than a video editor that also has voices.

## Who it's for

- **The faceless YouTube creator.** You publish narrated explainers and don't want to record your own voice.
- **The marketer repurposing content.** You turn blog posts and slide decks into short videos.
- **The course or training creator.** You need the same video in several languages.

It's a poor fit for people who want polished, cinematic AI footage. It's also wrong for detailed editing, because the document-style editor isn't a timeline.

## Where it's strong

**Stock-based video is cheap.** Stock and uploaded media cost no credits. You mainly pay for the voice, at 0.25 to 2 credits a minute, and 0.1 a minute for export.

**Published credit rates.** Fliki's guide lists what each action costs, down to the second, and that's more detail than many rivals share.

**Voice cloning on Standard.** You get one cloned voice at $28 a month, so your narration can sound like you.

**You keep your rights.** The terms say you retain your rights to what you create, and paid plans include a commercial licence. Free doesn't list one.

**A good long-term reputation.** Trustpilot shows 4.2 from 3,046 reviews, with 81% five-star and just 4% one-star. A rival's article reports 4.7 on both G2 and Capterra as of September 2026.

## What users complain about

That headline Trustpilot score needs context. Only 95 of those reviews came in the last 12 months, the profile merges reviews from others, and Fliki asks customers to leave them. Trustpilot also flags that it hasn't replied to negative reviews. So the score tells you more about the past than about this year.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated from November 2025 to October 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Support: 15.** Mostly tickets and emails with no answer.
2. **Billing or unexpected charges: 6.** Includes people saying they were charged on free accounts.
3. **Refunds refused or ignored: 5.**
4. **Poor output: 5.** Broken subtitles, gibberish text in images, weak voiceovers.
5. **Technical failures: 5.** Stuck exports and errors.
6. **Credits used on failed work: 2.**

Two more came from Fliki affiliates saying commissions weren't paid. That doesn't affect buyers much, but we're a Fliki affiliate ourselves, so you should know.

Rival Zebracat says that 10 of the latest 25 reviews sat at one to three stars in September, far above the all-time rate, and that failed generations in its own use still cost credits. It's selling a competing product, so treat that with care, but it does match what some Trustpilot reviewers describe.

**Refund and cancellation wording conflicts.** The FAQ says refunds can't be issued after you subscribe, while the terms say they're considered case by case. The pricing FAQ says you cancel through Manage Billing, while the terms say to contact support by email. So use Manage Billing, keep proof, and assume you won't get money back.

**Your content.** The terms let Fliki use your content to improve its services and develop new products. The privacy policy rules out AI training only for face media, so we'd assume nothing about the rest.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly, per month | Credits | Max export |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 3 | 5 minutes, 720p |
| Standard | $28 | $21 | 180 | 15 minutes, 1080p |
| Premium | $88 | $66 | 600 | 40 minutes, 1080p |

Yearly plans are paid up front, so that's $252 for Standard and $792 for Premium by our maths. One credit works out at 11 to 16 cents, depending on plan and billing.

Fliki's FAQ lists Premium's length as 30 minutes, while the pricing table says 40, so check if you need long videos.

Here's what 180 Standard credits buy, using Fliki's published rates:

| Video type | Credits per minute | Footage from 180 credits |
|---|---|---|
| Cheapest voice, stock | 0.35 | About 8.5 hours |
| Green voice, stock | 0.6 | About 5 hours |
| Yellow voice, stock | 1.1 | About 2.7 hours |
| Purple voice, stock | 2.1 | About 86 minutes |
| Stock avatar | 15 or more | About 12 minutes |
| AI video clips | 6 or more | 30 minutes at the very most |

These are best-case figures with no redo work. Fliki's own FAQ tells Standard users to expect 60 to 90 cumulative minutes, and that's the number we'd plan around. Edits to text trigger new voice generation, so rewriting your script costs more.

The bottom two rows are the trap. A single minute of avatar uses about 8% of your Standard month. AI clips vary widely by model, from 0.1 to 6 credits a second, and the top rates need Premium. Even there, 600 credits last under two minutes at the highest rate. If you want either regularly, plan for Premium and choose models carefully.

Unused credits disappear each month. Top-ups require an active paid plan and last 180 days. The FAQ also says renewing early can lose your remaining credits, so don't use that as a quick way to get more.

**Which plan should you pick?** Spend your free credits first, then go with Standard on monthly billing. Paying yearly saves $84, but with refunds this uncertain, wait until you've used it for a couple of months. Choose Premium only if you need AI clips, avatars or longer exports.

## Verdict

Fliki does one job very well: turning text into narrated videos in many languages, without a camera or a microphone. If that's your work, Standard is good value, and the published rates let you plan your month. Its weakness is what happens when something goes wrong. Support complaints dominate recent reviews, and refund wording is unfriendly. Stay on monthly billing until you trust it, and watch your credits the moment you add avatars or AI clips.

## FAQ

### Is Fliki free?

Yes. The free plan gives 3 credits a month, which Fliki describes as 5 minutes of content, with a watermark and 720p exports.

### Do Fliki credits roll over?

No. The monthly quota resets, and unused credits don't carry over. Top-up credits last 180 days.

### Can I use Fliki videos commercially?

Yes, on paid plans. Fliki's pricing FAQ says subscriptions include a commercial licence, provided your own content doesn't infringe copyright.

### Does Fliki give refunds?

Don't count on it. The FAQ says it can't refund after you subscribe, and the terms leave refunds to its discretion.

### How do I cancel Fliki?

Use Manage Billing in your account. The terms also list support@fliki.ai. You keep access until the paid period ends.
