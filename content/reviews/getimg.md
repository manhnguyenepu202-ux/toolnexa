## What getimg.ai actually is

getimg.ai has been running since 2022, and it's now a hub for other companies' AI models. You pick from image and video models from Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs, ByteDance, Kling and others, and pay with one credit balance. There are also tools for upscaling and editing.

It's run by Webrockets Sp. z o. o., a Polish company. The appeal is simple: one bill, one interface, and no need to sign up everywhere.

## Who it's for

- **The content creator.** You want a different model for each job.
- **The marketer.** You need product images and short ads, without a design team.
- **The model tourist.** You like comparing what each new release can do.

It's a poor fit for people who want a single, consistent look. It's also poor for long-time users who trained custom models, for reasons below.

## Where it's strong

**One account, many models.** This is the real value. Core and above get access to all models, and that saves juggling subscriptions.

**Up-front cost guides.** The pricing page shows what each plan buys, per model, in images and videos. Many rivals don't.

**Fair per-image pricing.** At about 13 cents for a premium image on any plan, there's no penalty for starting small.

**Your uploads stay yours.** The terms say getimg claims no rights over what you provide.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot shows 2.7 from just 11 reviews, with 64% one-star and only 7 in the last year. That's far too small to be a reliable score. getimg hasn't replied to negative reviews there.

We read all seven one-star reviews, dated June 2025 to April 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Cost and billing: 6.**
2. **Credits: 3.** Rising costs, and a 6-second video that took 960 credits on an older plan.
3. **Output quality: 2.**
4. **Moderation: 2.** Filters that seem random, blocking harmless images.
5. **Support: 2.**
6. **Technical problems: 2.**

**Failed images can still cost you.** A Trustpilot reviewer and at least one independent review both report fully black images being charged. Check your balance if you hit one, and contact support.

**The 2026 overhaul upset existing users.** Third-party reviews say the free plan was retired, and that custom model training and community models were removed in March 2026. Those reviews come from rivals, but a March Trustpilot review says the same thing about custom models. If you need your own trained model, look elsewhere.

**Old, conflicting legal pages.** The terms were last updated in November 2022, and they don't really describe today's product. One FAQ says commercial use is allowed on paid plans under a CreativeML licence, while another says everything you create can be used commercially, subject to the terms. Different models may carry different rules, so check before running a major campaign.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly, per month | Credits | Premium images | UGC videos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | $15 | About $11 | 8,000 | 114 | 26 |
| Core | $35 | About $26 | 19,000 | 271 | 63 |
| Plus | $79 | About $58 | 42,500 | 607 | 141 |
| Ultra | $199 | About $147 | 107,500 | 1,535 | 358 |

Yearly figures are our estimate from the 26% saving, so confirm them at checkout. Image and video counts come from getimg's page, using Nano Banana 2 and its UGC video option. Other models cost more or less.

The maths is flat. A premium image costs about 13 cents on every plan, and a UGC video about 58 cents. So buying a bigger plan gets you more credits and faster batches, but not a better rate.

The real choice is between Entry and Core. Entry only includes selected models, so if you're here for model choice, you'll want Core at $35. Credits don't roll over, so don't buy more than you'll use.

The page says top-ups are on all plans, and its FAQ says they're for Plus and Ultra, once you've used 90% of your credits. So don't count on topping up at a lower plan. API use is billed separately.

**Read the refund terms closely.** You can only get a refund if you've used under 100 credits and it's within 3 days of starting or renewing. That's a very small window. Promotional or discounted purchases aren't refundable at all, and that may include sale prices.

**Which plan should you pick?** With no free plan, start on Entry monthly, as a paid trial. Use your first 100 credits carefully, because that keeps your refund open for three days. Move to Core if you hit a model you can't use.

## Verdict

getimg.ai is a sensible way to reach many AI models from one place, and at $15 it's a fair experiment. We'd be careful about going further. There's no free plan, the refund rules are among the tightest we've seen, the legal terms are years out of date, and support isn't visible. So treat it as a month-to-month utility, not a home for your whole workflow. If you know the one model you want, go straight to it.

## FAQ

### Is getimg.ai free?

We found no free plan on the pricing page. Reviews say it ended in the 2026 overhaul, and older articles offering free credits are out of date.

### Can I use getimg.ai images commercially?

Yes on paid plans, according to its FAQ, though two FAQs word this differently. Check the rules for your model.

### Do getimg.ai credits roll over?

No. Plan credits expire at the end of the month.

### Can I get a refund?

Only if you've used under 100 credits and it's within 3 days of starting or renewing. Promotional purchases are excluded.

### Can I train my own model?

Reviews say custom training was removed in March 2026. Check the app before you subscribe for that.
