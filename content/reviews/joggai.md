## What JoggAI actually is

JoggAI started as an avatar video tool and now does much more. You can paste a product URL or script, pick an avatar, and get a short ad with a voiceover. There are also product photo and video tools, translation, talking photos, and access to AI models such as Seedance through one credit balance. Credits are shared between the app, API and MCP.

The company behind it is JOGG PTE. LTD. in Singapore. Most of its pitch is aimed at people who'd otherwise pay human creators for UGC-style ads.

## Who it's for

- **The ecommerce seller.** You want to test many ad angles for one product without hiring creators.
- **The small marketing team.** You need quick avatar videos for social or landing pages.
- **The developer.** Credits work across the API too, so you don't need a separate plan.

It's a poor fit for brands who need a unique face, since stock avatars aren't exclusive to you. It's also a poor fit for anyone who won't check the AI's work, because output quality is a common complaint.

## Where it's strong

**Low cost per minute.** Standard avatar video works out at about 24 cents a minute on Starter, and that's the best thing about it. Our [JoggAI vs Creatify comparison](/compare/joggai-vs-creatify) shows how large that gap can be.

**Editing is free.** JoggAI's credit guide says editing and previewing don't use credits, which rewards checking your work before you render.

**Rollover on monthly plans.** Unused credits last up to three months, so a slow month isn't wasted.

**You keep your rights.** The terms say you own your content, including commercial use, except for any parts owned by JoggAI or its licensors.

**Monthly cancellation is in the app.** JoggAI documents the steps, and that's not true of every tool we've covered.

## What users complain about

Trustpilot shows 2.1 from just 33 reviews, with 21% five-star and 64% one-star. Only 11 came in the last 12 months, so this is a small sample, and small samples skew towards unhappy people. Product Hunt is kinder, at 4.0 from 20 reviews, some of them complaining about changes to lifetime-deal credits.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated March 2025 to October 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Support: 8.** Includes people saying replies stopped.
2. **Credits: 7.** Costs or balances that didn't match what they expected.
3. **Output quality: 7.**
4. **Refunds refused: 6.**
5. **Technical failures: 6.** Failed renders are the main example.
6. **Cancelling: 5.**
7. **Billing: 4.**

JoggAI replied to 40% of negative reviews, but Trustpilot says that typically takes over a month. In those replies it says refunds aren't part of its structure, and one rejected a refund request made six hours after purchase, according to the reviewer.

**The credit system keeps changing.** Pricing reviews from August 2026 list plans with a few hundred credits a year. The current page shows 12,000 a month. A September reviewer said they paid $29 and got 16 credits, which we can't explain. A rival says the August figures were replaced in October. Older reviews also promise unlimited video, which the current pricing page doesn't offer. So only trust what you see on the page today.

**Docs disagree.** The credit guide says avatar video costs 25 credits per 15 seconds, and the pricing page's 120 minutes fits that. The API docs list 250. If you're building on the API, test a short job first. The pricing FAQ and table also disagree on whether Team has two or three seats.

**Your content.** The terms let JoggAI use your content to improve its services, and let other users share or use it through service features. That second part deserves attention if your scripts or product footage are sensitive.

## A warning on fake testimonials

This matters more than any feature. In the US, the FTC's rule on reviews bans testimonials from people who don't exist, including AI-generated ones. An AI avatar reading a brand message is one thing. An avatar saying "this cream cleared my skin" is a made-up customer. Keep avatars in a presenter role, and don't use them to fake personal experience. If you sell in other countries, check the rules there too.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly, per month | Credits | Avatar video |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 200, valid 31 days | About 1 minute |
| Starter | $29 | $24 | 12,000 a month | About 120 minutes |
| Creator 1 | $49 | $40.66 | 22,000 a month | About 220 minutes |
| Team | Not shown | $215 | 1,320,000 a year | About 13,200 minutes |

Yearly billing saves $60 on Starter and $100 on Creator 1, but you pay $288 or $488 up front, and credits arrive all at once. Higher Creator tiers exist too.

Here's what Starter's 12,000 credits buy, using JoggAI's published rates:

| Work | Credits | Output from 12,000 |
|---|---|---|
| 30-second standard avatar ad | 50 | 240 ads |
| Translation, per language, 30 seconds | 50 | 240 versions |
| Talking photo, up to 1 minute | 200 | 60 clips |
| 10-second Seedance 2.5 clip | About 1,200 | About 10 clips |
| Video face swap, 10 seconds | 500 | 24 clips |

Credits round up to the next 15-second block, so a 16-second video costs the same as a 30-second one. Trim your scripts to fit.

The gap between rows is the main thing to understand. Standard avatars are very cheap. Generated footage costs around 24 times as much per clip as a 30-second avatar ad, even though it's far shorter. If your ads depend on Seedance, you'll get about 100 seconds of footage a month from Starter.

**Which plan should you pick?** Use the free credits first, and then buy Starter on monthly billing. Refunds aren't available, and rollover applies to monthly plans, so there's little reason to prepay. Move to Creator 1 only if you need the 5-minute AvatarX limit or more avatars.

## Your first hour

1. Use the free credits on a short, real script.
2. Watch the avatar's mouth closely and read every on-screen word.
3. Compare the credit estimate with what's actually deducted.
4. Paste in a product URL and check the claims it writes for you.
5. Find Cancel Plan before paying. Deleting your account doesn't cancel.

## Look elsewhere when

- You need strong support or a safety net on refunds.
- You want a unique on-screen face. Hire a creator, or look at custom avatars.
- Your ads depend on AI-generated footage. Compare per-clip costs first.
- You're in a regulated field, such as health or finance.

## Verdict

JoggAI's headline value is real. If you need lots of short avatar videos, it looks far cheaper than Creatify, its closest rival. But a 2.1 Trustpilot score from a small sample is still a warning, a refund isn't realistically available, and its credit system changed a lot in 2026. So we'd use it as a low-risk monthly experiment. Pay $29, make your videos, and judge it on what comes out. Don't prepay a year until you've seen a few months of consistent results.

## FAQ

### Is JoggAI free?

There's a free offer of 200 credits, valid for 31 days. That's about a minute of avatar video, so it's a trial rather than a working plan.

### Does JoggAI have a watermark?

Watermark removal is listed from Starter up, so expect one on free output.

### Does JoggAI give refunds?

The terms say payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires it. Treat every payment as final.

### How do I cancel JoggAI?

Click the credit icon, open Subscriptions, click the arrow next to Upgrade, and choose Cancel Plan. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

### Do JoggAI credits roll over?

On monthly plans, unused credits roll over for up to three months. Free credits expire after 31 days.

### Can I use AI avatars for UGC ads?

Yes, but don't present them as real customers. The FTC's rule bans testimonials from people who don't exist.
