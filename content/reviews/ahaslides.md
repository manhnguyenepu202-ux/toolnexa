## Key takeaways

- **What it is:** live polls, quizzes, word clouds and Q&A inside slides that your audience answers on their phones.
- **What it really costs:** free for up to 50 people, then $107.40 a year for 100 people or $191.40 a year for 2,500. Monthly billing is about three times the rate.
- **Best for:** teachers, trainers and small teams running regular sessions for under 100 people.
- **Skip it if:** you present to large crowds on a budget, or you can't accept a vendor that lost account data in 2026.
- **Our score:** 7.8/10. Good value and well liked, held back by a recent breach and unclear plan limits.

## What AhaSlides actually is

You build a deck, add interactive slides, and your audience joins from their phones. Answers appear on screen as they come in. It covers the usual set of polls, quizzes, word clouds, open questions and Q&A.

You don't have to leave your existing slides behind. The help docs describe PowerPoint and PDF import, a PowerPoint add-in that works on every plan, a Google Slides add-on, and tools for Zoom and Teams.

The company is AhaSlides Pte. Ltd., based in Singapore. What sets it apart from Mentimeter is mostly price. It isn't the prettiest option, and users say so.

## Who it's for

- **The teacher with a class of 35.** The free plan may be enough. If not, Edu Small costs about $35 a year.
- **The corporate trainer.** You run workshops for 20 to 80 people each month, and Essential fits that well.
- **The team lead.** You want Q&A and quick polls at a monthly all-hands, and you can tolerate a slightly plain look.

## Where it's strong

**User ratings are good.** Capterra shows 4.6 from 67 reviews, with 95% positive sentiment. The sub-scores we could see were 4.7 for ease of use, 4.4 for value and 4.3 for customer service. G2 figures we saw in search results and on AhaSlides' site range from 4.6 to 4.7, from over 300 reviews, though we couldn't open G2 itself.

**The free plan does real work.** You get up to 50 people at once, unlimited presentations and PowerPoint import. Mentimeter's free plan counts 50 participants per month, not per session, so a weekly class would use that up quickly.

**The participant limit is kinder than it sounds.** AhaSlides counts people connected at the same time. If someone leaves, their place is freed.

**Education pricing is unusually low.** Edu Small is $2.95 a month, billed yearly. That's a very small bill for a tool used every week.

**Your data isn't used to train AI.** The privacy policy says user data doesn't train AI models, and that third-party AI services keep inputs only as long as needed for processing.

## What users complain about, and what went wrong

**1. The 2026 data breach.** This comes first because it's the thing to weigh most. AhaSlides says an intruder used a flaw in Metabase, an internal analytics tool, and had access from August 12 to September 7, 2026. It says this affected all registered AhaSlides.com accounts.

The exposed data was email addresses, signup IP and city, invoice details (name, purchase IP, what was bought) and hashed passwords. Billing addresses were exposed for 0.12% of users. AhaSlides says card numbers and plain-text passwords weren't exposed, and the separate AhaSlides.eu deployment wasn't touched.

In its favour, the notice is specific and tells people what to do: reset your password, change it anywhere you reused it, and be wary of emails that mention your purchases. The company says it's undergoing SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits, which means those aren't finished. A one-star Trustpilot review from September 11 says only that data was leaked.

**2. Hidden free plan limits.** AhaSlides' docs say limits on quiz and unscored slides aren't listed on the My Plan page, and you find out from the Present button. Go over them, and your session drops to a small, test-sized audience. Some free accounts also have a monthly participant allowance. Third-party sites disagree here: one says 5 quiz and 3 poll questions per presentation, another says unlimited. Essential lists unlimited interactive slides as an upgrade, so a cap clearly exists. Check your deck with the Present button days before the session.

**3. Design and formatting.** Rigid layouts are the most common complaint in one secondary summary of G2 reviews, though we couldn't check the count ourselves. One Trustpilot reviewer also wanted to change bar chart colours on results. Don't expect Canva polish.

**4. Gaps for specific users.** One independent review notes no Webex panel and no native grade sync with Canvas, Blackboard or Brightspace. Another mentions lag on poor connections and strict corporate networks. Export is paid only.

**5. Thin public support record.** Trustpilot shows just 7 reviews, a 3.1 score, and a badge saying the company hasn't replied to negative ones. That's too few to mean much. One happy reviewer this year praised the support bot and the human help behind it, which fits Capterra's solid but lower 4.3.

We could only see the title of a one-star Capterra review from October 2026, so we can't say what it's about.

**Real scenarios:**

The team figure comes from AhaSlides' own example: $574.20 less 10%. The banner says savings run up to 20%, so ask at larger sizes.

**The fine print doesn't agree with itself.** The pricing page promises a full refund within 14 days, as long as you haven't used AhaSlides at a live event. The terms say there are no refunds for unused billing periods and don't mention that promise. The FAQ says you keep your benefits until the plan expires after cancelling, while the terms say cancellation takes effect immediately and content is deleted. The privacy policy, in turn, says content is kept until you delete it. Export anything important before you cancel, and cancel at least an hour before renewal, as the terms state.

**Which plan should you pick?** Start on Free and test your actual deck. Teachers should move to Edu Small if the slide limits bite. Paid users under 100 people want Essential yearly. Only buy Pro if you need the larger audience, AI features, branding or Q&A moderation. AI appears as a Pro feature on the pricing page while the docs mention free AI assistance, and we found no published quota.

## Your first hour

1. Sign up with a unique password. If you already have an account, reset it now.
2. Import your existing deck, or use the PowerPoint add-in.
3. Add a word cloud, a poll and two quiz slides.
4. Press Present and see if a limit warning appears.
5. Join from a phone on mobile data, to check how it behaves away from office Wi-Fi.
6. Check your My Plan page for your real limits.

## Look elsewhere when

- Your audiences are often over 100 and the budget is tight. Mentimeter Basic is cheaper.
- You need grade sync with your LMS. Check this before you buy.
- Your company's security review needs finished certifications. Ask for the SOC 2 and ISO status first.
- You run one annual event and want no subscription. Look at Slido's one-time plans.

## Verdict

For regular sessions under 100 people, AhaSlides is a sensible choice, and it's great value for teachers. We'd start on the free plan and pay yearly only once a real deck works. The breach doesn't rule it out, since the notice lists no card data and no presentations among what was taken. It should change how you sign up, though: use a unique password, and be careful with billing emails. Larger crowds should compare Mentimeter Basic first, because at that size the cheaper tool isn't this one.

## FAQ

### Is AhaSlides free?

Yes, for up to 50 people at once, with no time limit and no card. Limits on quiz and other slides apply, and the docs don't publish them.

### Is AhaSlides safe after the 2026 breach?

AhaSlides says it patched the flaw and tightened access. Email, invoice and hashed password data for all AhaSlides.com accounts was exposed, so reset your password and watch for phishing.

### Does AhaSlides have refunds?

The pricing page offers a full refund within 14 days if you haven't used it at a live event. The terms are stricter, so ask in writing and keep the reply.

### Can I use AhaSlides in PowerPoint?

Yes. The add-in works on every plan with Microsoft 365 and PowerPoint 2019 or later, including the web version.

### Is AhaSlides cheaper than Mentimeter?

Under 100 people, yes. For larger groups, Mentimeter Basic costs less than AhaSlides Pro, though Pro includes branding.
