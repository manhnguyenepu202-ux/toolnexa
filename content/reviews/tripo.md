## Key takeaways

- **What it is:** an AI generator that turns images and text into 3D models, with texturing, retopology and auto-rigging.
- **What it really costs:** $20 or $90 a month, or $110 per seat for Team. A fully textured, rigged model on Pro works out at about $0.43, not the headline price.
- **Best for:** indie developers, 3D artists and hobbyists who need lots of models quickly.
- **Skip it if:** you need animation-ready characters without cleanup, or you're expecting a generous refund policy.
- **Our score:** 8.3/10. Strong value and speed, let down by billing complaints and silent support.

## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based review, without generating models of our own. We read Tripo's pricing page, help centre and terms, its Trustpilot profile and 20 most recent one-star reviews, G2 data through search summaries, and several third-party tests. Everything was checked on November 5, 2026.

We should be upfront about the evidence on quality. We found no neutral benchmark. The tests we found are run by rival vendors or single bloggers, so treat quality claims as a guide.

## What Tripo actually is

You upload an image or type a prompt, and Tripo builds a 3D model in seconds. You can then texture it, split it into parts, reduce its polygon count and rig it for animation. Bridges connect to Blender, Unity, Unreal, Maya and others, so models can move straight into your existing tools.

Tripo is run by Holymolly Ltd, and its terms fall under Hong Kong law. That's worth knowing if your legal team reads such things.

## Who it's for

- **The indie game developer.** You need dozens of props and background assets, and a little cleanup is fine.
- **The 3D artist.** You want a fast starting mesh to refine in Blender, not a finished hero asset.
- **The hobbyist with a 3D printer.** You want a printable figure from a picture. One tester's mesh came out watertight with inconsistent face winding, so plan a repair pass.

It's a poor fit for a studio that needs production-ready animated characters straight from the tool. Reviewers keep saying these meshes need work first.

## Where it's strong

**Speed.** Fast generation is the one thing nearly every source agrees on. Comparison guides routinely name Tripo as the quickest of the main tools.

**More models for the money.** At $20, Pro's 3,000 credits go further than Meshy's 1,000 at the same price. On the published rates, that's about twice as many finished models.

**Fair failure rules.** The help centre says credits are only deducted on success, and failed jobs return them within an hour.

**Purchased credits last.** Extra credits never expire, even if you cancel. Monthly credits are different: they reset, and they're cleared if you cancel.

**Rigging included.** Auto-rigging costs 20 credits, and guides often name Tripo first for game-ready workflows, though not everyone agrees the results animate well.

## What users complain about

Tripo's Trustpilot score is 4.3 from 510 reviews, with 81% five-star and 11% one-star. Almost all of them, 506, came in the last 12 months, since the profile was claimed in August 2025. G2 search summaries show 4.7 from 216 reviews for Tripo Studio. Those are good numbers, though one reviewer alleges credits were offered for five-star reviews. We couldn't confirm that.

We read the 20 latest one-star reviews, dated August 7 to November 3, 2026. Themes overlap:

1. **Cancelling and refunds: about 9.** Reviewers describe a hidden or broken cancel path, or refunds refused.
2. **Unexpected charges: about 6.** Includes charges after cancelling and double charges.
3. **Output quality: about 6.** Models unusable for animation, or poor low-poly results.
4. **Paid gates: about 4.** Reviewers didn't expect to need a subscription to download or export.
5. **Support: about 4.** Slow or unhelpful replies. Trustpilot's badge says the company hasn't replied to negative reviews.
6. **Credits not rolling over: 2.**

The billing theme matches what we saw at rivals, including Meshy, so it's partly a category problem. That doesn't make it fine.

**Topology is the real limit.** Reviewers broadly agree that meshes need cleanup for animation. Quad remeshing gives even polygons, not the edge loops characters need to deform well. One tester also found image input gave cleaner geometry than text prompts, so start from a good reference picture.

## Pricing: what you'll really pay

| Plan | Price | Credits | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 200 | No, models are public |
| Pro | $20/mo | 3,000 | Yes, private models |
| Max | $90/mo | 25,000 | Yes, private models |
| Team | $110/seat, or $55 on annual (3 seats shown) | 90,000 | Yes, shared workspace |

The page says Pro gives "≈ 200 models". That assumes bare generation only, at 15 credits each. A usable model costs more.

Tripo's own pages disagree on the base cost: 15 credits on the pricing page, and 25 in the help centre. We use 25 below to stay on the safe side:

| Step | Credits |
|---|---|
| Generate | 25 |
| Texture | +10 |
| HD texture | +10 |
| Auto rig | +20 |
| **Total** | **65** |

That's about 46 finished models on Pro, or $0.43 each. Even on the 15-credit figure, you get about 54. One tester reported 55 credits for a default run, which fits. Tripo's page also says $0.16 per model, a figure that doesn't match its own 200-model claim, so we'd ignore both.

Here's how that plays out:

| Who | Monthly work | Credits | Plan | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hobbyist printing figures | 10 untextured models | 250 | Pro | $20 |
| Indie developer | 40 textured, rigged assets | 2,600 | Pro | $20 |
| Small studio | 400 textured models | 18,000 | Max | $90 |

Four things to know:

- **Free is for trying only.** You get public, non-commercial models and limited exports. Check the export limit before you start a project.
- **Max is the value jump.** Credits cost $3.60 per 1,000 against $6.67 on Pro and $10 for top-ups. Past about 10,000 credits a month, Max beats Pro plus top-ups ($20 + 7,000 credits at $10 per 1,000 = $90).
- **The annual discount is unclear.** The page shows "50% off", yet Pro and Max displayed the same price in monthly and annual views. Check checkout before you commit.
- **Refunds are narrow.** The help centre allows one within 24 hours for a mistaken purchase, if no credits were used. The terms say fees are otherwise non-refundable.

**Which plan should you pick?** Pro, monthly. Move to Max only once you're regularly buying top-ups.

## Tripo vs rivals

**Meshy** is the main alternative. It has a stronger Trustpilot record and more printing tools, but fewer credits for your money. Pick Meshy if you mainly 3D print or value its reputation. See our [Tripo vs Meshy comparison](/compare/tripo-vs-meshy).

**Rodin** is often praised for fine detail, at a higher reported cost. Pick Rodin if single hero assets matter more than volume.

**Hunyuan3D** is the open option. Pick Hunyuan3D if you want to run models on your own hardware.

## Your first hour

1. Start with a clean, front-facing image rather than a text prompt.
2. Use free credits to check the mesh in Blender at close range.
3. Test-rig a character if you plan to animate, and watch how joints deform.
4. Note the total credits for one finished model, then multiply by your monthly output.
5. If you subscribe, find the cancel option (credits balance, Membership tab, My Plan) and set a renewal reminder.

## Look elsewhere when

- You need clean edge loops for hero characters. A human artist still wins.
- Your priority is print-ready output. Compare Meshy's print tools.
- You need exclusive outputs. Tripo's terms say others may receive similar results.
- You want a longer refund window. Meshy's runs five days, with conditions.

## Verdict

Tripo is one of the better deals in AI 3D. It's quick, the credit rules are fair when jobs fail, and $20 buys a useful number of finished models. Just budget for $0.43 a model rather than the headline figure, plan on cleanup for anything that has to animate, and keep an eye on your subscription. For props, prototypes and background assets, we'd happily start here. For hero characters, treat it as a first draft.

## FAQ

### Is Tripo AI free?

Yes, there's a free plan with 200 credits a month, but models are public and non-commercial, and exports are limited.

### Can I use Tripo models commercially?

Paid users generally retain rights to their models and can use them commercially. Free plan use is non-commercial.

### How many models do you really get on Pro?

About 46 fully textured, rigged models by our math, rather than the roughly 200 bare models on the pricing page.

### Do Tripo credits expire?

Monthly credits reset and are cleared if you cancel. Credits you buy separately never expire.

### Are Tripo models good enough for animation?

They can be a good start, but most reviewers say characters need topology and UV cleanup first.
