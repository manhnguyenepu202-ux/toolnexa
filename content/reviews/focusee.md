## What FocuSee actually is

You record your screen, with or without your webcam. FocuSee tracks where you click and type, then adds smooth zooms to those spots automatically. It also enlarges and smooths the cursor, adds a background and rounded frame, and can generate captions. The result looks like a video someone spent an hour keyframing.

It's made by iMobie and runs on both Windows and Mac, which matters because its best-known rival, Screen Studio, is Mac-only.

## Who it's for

- **SaaS teams** recording product walkthroughs and feature launches.
- **Course creators** making software tutorials.
- **Marketers** cutting vertical clips for social media from one recording.
- **Windows users** who want Screen Studio-style results.

## Where it's strong

**Automatic zoom.** The main reason to buy it. Clicks become smooth zooms without any editing.

**Cross-platform.** Windows and Mac, unlike Screen Studio.

**Quick exports for several formats.** Landscape for YouTube and vertical for Shorts, Reels and TikTok from the same recording.

**Refund window.** FocuSee states a 60-day money-back guarantee on annual and lifetime plans, which is generous for this category. Monthly plans have a much shorter window, and used AI credits may be deducted.

## What users complain about

- **Bugs and export issues.** Some Trustpilot reviewers report crashes, audio drifting out of sync or slow exports.
- **Confusing pricing.** Different official pages show different yearly prices and plan names.
- **Limited trial.** The free trial exports one watermarked video, so you can't fully test it before paying.
- **Editing depth.** It's a recorder with smart effects, not a replacement for a full editor.

## FocuSee vs rivals

**Screen Studio** is the best-known auto-zoom recorder, but it runs only on Mac.

**Loom** is better for quick async messages and sharing links, with less polish on the video itself.

**Camtasia** gives far more editing control, at a higher price and with a steeper learning curve.

## Your first hour

1. Download the trial and record a 60-second walkthrough of an app you know well.
2. Watch the automatic zooms and adjust any that feel too fast.
3. Export the one trial video in the format you'll use most.
4. Check audio sync on the full video before you buy.
5. If you buy, choose a yearly or lifetime plan to get the 60-day refund window.

## Look elsewhere when
