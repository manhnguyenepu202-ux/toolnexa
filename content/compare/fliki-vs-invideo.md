## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without making videos of our own. We read pricing pages, credit docs and terms for both tools, plus their Trustpilot profiles, including the 20 latest one-star reviews for each. Checked November 5, 2026.

## The short answer

Pick by the kind of video you make. For narrated videos using stock footage, especially in several languages, Fliki is the better and more predictable tool. For generated footage and AI editing, InVideo does more. Most people making everyday content will have an easier time on Fliki.

## Choose Fliki if

- Your videos are mainly voiceover plus stock clips.
- You need many voices or languages.
- You want to clone your own voice on a mid-priced plan.
- You want a simple, script-first editor.

## Choose InVideo if

- You want generated footage, not stock.
- You'd like an AI agent to draft and revise for you.
- You want a timeline, with edits that don't use credits.
- You're comfortable watching credits closely.

## The real differences

**How they spend credits.** This is the big one. Fliki charges mostly for voice, and stock media costs nothing, so a narrated video is cheap and predictable. InVideo charges for generation, and its agent can spend credits on your behalf. That helps explain why credit complaints dominate its recent reviews and barely feature in Fliki's.

**Editing.** InVideo has a multitrack timeline, and timeline edits and exports are free. Fliki uses a scene-based editor, and exports cost 0.1 credits a minute. Changing text also creates new voice work, so edits aren't free in the same way.

**AI generation.** InVideo gives access to many AI models and is built around them. Fliki offers AI clips too, at 0.1 to 6 credits a second, but its best models are reserved for Premium.

**Voices.** Fliki is stronger here, with 80+ languages and 1,000 voices on Standard.

## What the money buys

Here's a rough idea of what a month gets you on the plans most people would compare.

| Goal | Fliki Standard | InVideo Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Narrated stock video | 60 to 90 minutes, per Fliki's FAQ | Depends on agent use |
| Generated 720p footage | Varies by model | About 3 minutes |
| Generated 1080p footage | Premium-only on some models | About 1 minute 44 seconds |

So if you need narration in bulk, Fliki gives you more video for the money. If you want generated footage, InVideo's Plus plan is the better buy at $60 a month, against $88 for Fliki Premium.

## Refunds and cancelling

InVideo offers a seven-day refund window on unused plans, so it has the clearer offer, as long as you haven't spent credits. Fliki's FAQ says it can't refund once you subscribe, while its terms allow case-by-case exceptions. Neither is generous, but InVideo gives you a real, if narrow, way out.

Whichever you pick, start monthly. You'll learn your real credit use in one billing cycle.

## Switching

Both tools produce finished video files, so you keep those. Projects don't move between them. Scripts are easy to reuse, so keep yours in a separate document. Spend or write off your credits before leaving, and cancel ahead of your renewal date.

## Verdict

Pick Fliki if you publish narrated videos on a schedule. It's cheaper to run for that job and much easier to predict, and we scored it higher overall at 7.8 against 7.3. Pick InVideo if generated footage and agent editing are the point, and set a firm limit on credit use. If you're unsure, write one real script and try it on both free plans.

## FAQ

### Is Fliki better than InVideo?

For narrated stock videos, yes. For generated footage and timeline editing, InVideo is stronger.

### Which is cheaper?

InVideo's entry price is lower, at $10 a month on yearly billing against Fliki's $21. For bulk narration, Fliki usually goes further.

### Which has a better refund policy?

InVideo, with a seven-day window on unused plans. Fliki's FAQ says it doesn't refund after you subscribe.

### Do credits roll over on either?

No. Monthly credits don't carry over on either tool.
