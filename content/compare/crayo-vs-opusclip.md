## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without making videos of our own. We read Crayo's pricing, terms and refund policy, drew on our earlier research into OpusClip's pricing and help centre, and read both Trustpilot profiles. Crayo was checked on November 6, 2026, and OpusClip on November 5.

## The short answer

These tools overlap on clipping, and on not much else. If you have long videos of your own, use OpusClip. It's built for that job, has a better reputation, and gives some refund protection. Choose Crayo only if you specifically want Reddit-story or fake-text formats.

## Choose Crayo if

- You want Reddit-story, fake-text or gameplay-style shorts.
- You have no long video to start from.
- You don't mind downloading and posting by hand.

## Choose OpusClip if

- You've got podcasts, interviews, webinars or long YouTube videos.
- You want the tool to find the best moments for you.
- You want scheduling and professional export options.
- You want a lower entry price.

## The real differences

**Starting material.** This is the main question. OpusClip needs a long video. Crayo can start from a story or a script, and that's why faceless channels like it.

**What you're paying for.** OpusClip charges for source minutes uploaded, whether you keep one clip or thirty. Crayo charges through four meters: workflow credits, export minutes, voiceover minutes and AI images. Both models take a little budgeting, but in Crayo's case the smallest meter is often the one that stops you.

**Finding good clips.** OpusClip's point is selection, with a virality score to rank clips. AutoClip does a similar job in Crayo, but we found far less independent evidence on how well it works.

**Posting.** OpusClip offers social scheduling. Third-party reviews say Crayo requires you to download and post manually.

**Exports for editors.** OpusClip offers Premiere and DaVinci export, which suits people who finish clips in a real editor.

Crayo's latest 20 one-star reviews lead with billing (about 12), support (about 10) and refunds (about 9). About 8 mention a $5 course or a third-party offer requiring a subscription. Crayo says it has no such course. It's a confusing situation, and a good reason to check exactly what you're buying.

## Refunds and legal terms

Crayo's refund policy is as strict as they come: all sales are final, even accidental purchases. OpusClip's terms also say fees already paid aren't refunded, but Pro users can refund an undownloaded project within 14 days. That's credits rather than cash, but it's still something.

Crayo also has a conflict between its FAQ, which says you own your videos, and its 2024 terms, which mention personal, non-commercial use. Businesses should get a written answer before relying on Crayo.

## YouTube monetization

Neither tool makes your videos eligible for ad revenue. YouTube's policy excludes mass-produced and template-based content, and names generic AI content. That's a bigger risk for Crayo's formats, since repetition is built in. Clips of your own long videos are less likely to fall under that, as long as you have the rights to them.

## Switching

There's nothing to migrate, because both produce finished video files. If you move from Crayo to OpusClip, you'll need a long video to work with. If you go the other way, you'll need new story content. Download your work first, since OpusClip warns projects go out of reach three days after cancelling, and Crayo reserves the right to delete data on cancellation.

## Verdict

If you already make long videos, pick OpusClip. It's cheaper, better reviewed, and made for that job, which is why we score it at 8.2 against Crayo's 7.0. Pick Crayo only for its story and fake-text formats, on a single monthly plan. If you're not sure which you are, you probably don't need Crayo.

## FAQ

### Is Crayo better than OpusClip?

Only for Reddit-story and fake-text videos. For clipping long videos, OpusClip is the stronger choice.

### Which is cheaper?

OpusClip, at $15 a month against $29. Crayo's $19 price is a quarterly plan.

### Do they offer refunds?

Crayo doesn't. OpusClip's Pro plan allows refunds on undownloaded projects within 14 days, and paid fees generally aren't returned.

### Can I earn from YouTube with these?

Possibly, but YouTube requires original, non-repetitive content. Neither tool guarantees monetization.
