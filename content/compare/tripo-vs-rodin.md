## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without generating models ourselves. We read both pricing pages and refund terms, both Trustpilot profiles, an independent blind test published in 2026, and our own [Tripo review](/tools/tripo). Checked November 15, 2026.

## The short answer

**Tripo** is the better buy for most people: cheaper per model, faster, with auto-rigging, and it won an independent blind test on raw geometry. **Rodin** is worth the higher price when you need a few hero assets with native quad topology and 4K PBR textures.

## Choose Tripo if

- You need lots of models on a budget.
- You want automatic rigging for characters.
- You print, prototype or make background game props.

## Choose Rodin if

- You need clean quad meshes for animation or a studio pipeline.
- Texture detail matters more than volume.
- You want an API or plugins for Blender, Unity or Unreal.

## What an independent test found

A developer ran Meshy 6, Tripo v3.1 and Rodin Gen-2.5 through each vendor's public API in April and May 2026, and had reviewers vote on outputs without knowing which tool made them. On untextured ("white") meshes, **Tripo beat Rodin in 77.9% of single-image votes and 62.7% of multi-image votes**. Rodin produced the most polygons, but more faces didn't mean better votes.

That's one test with one set of subjects, so treat it as a useful signal rather than a final verdict. Rodin's strength is fine surface detail and texture realism, which a white-mesh vote doesn't measure.

## The real differences

**Topology.** Rodin outputs native quad meshes, which riggers and game engines prefer. Tripo offers retopology tools, and the blind test favoured its overall geometry.

**Textures.** Rodin aims for 4K PBR materials on higher plans. Tripo's textures are good for props and prototypes.

**Workflow.** Rodin lets you preview results and spend credits only on downloads. Tripo spends credits as you generate, and offers auto-rigging and part segmentation.

**Export formats.** Both export the common formats, including GLB, FBX, OBJ and STL. Tripo also exports USD and 3MF for color printing.

## What users complain about

- **Rodin:** Trustpilot reviews mention difficulty cancelling subscriptions and slow refund replies. Hyper3D replies to negative reviews, but its terms say payments are non-refundable except where the law requires.
- **Tripo:** recent reviews also include cancellation and charge complaints, and the headline credit counts ignore texture and rigging costs.

For either tool, cancel well before renewal and keep screenshots.

## Verdict

For most indie developers, hobbyists and 3D printing fans, **Tripo** gives more usable models for the money. If you're producing a handful of hero assets and your pipeline needs clean quads and detailed PBR textures, **Rodin** justifies its higher price. Many studios use both: Tripo for volume, Rodin for the pieces the camera sees up close.

## FAQ

### Is Tripo or Rodin better?

Tripo is better value and won an independent blind test on raw geometry. Rodin is better for quad topology and detailed textures.

### Is Rodin more expensive than Tripo?

Yes. Rodin Creator is $30 a month ($24 billed yearly) for about 60 models; Tripo Pro is about $20 a month and usually cheaper per model.

### Does Rodin offer refunds?

Hyper3D's policy says payments are final and non-refundable except where applicable law requires. Use the free credits or trial first.

### Which is better for 3D printing?

Tripo, because it exports STL and 3MF and costs less per model. For multicolor printing, also consider Meshy.
