## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without running either tool on our own meetings. We read both pricing pages and help centres, G2, App Store and Trustpilot reviews, and independent write-ups. Checked November 15, 2026.

## The short answer

Pick **Krisp** if noisy calls are your problem and you want notes without a bot in the meeting. Pick **Notta** if you transcribe lots of recordings, work in several languages, and want the lowest yearly price, but protect yourself on billing.

## Choose Krisp if

- Background noise or other voices are hurting your calls.
- Clients or your company don't allow meeting bots.
- You mostly hold meetings in English.
- You want a usable free plan for daily calls.

## Choose Notta if

- You need transcription in many languages or bilingual meetings.
- You transcribe uploaded audio and video, not just live calls.
- You record in-person conversations on your phone.
- Price per minute matters most.

## The real differences

| | Krisp | Notta |
|---|---|---|
| Main job | Noise cancellation + meeting notes | Transcription + summaries |
| Joins meetings as a bot | No | Yes |
| Languages | Fewer, English-first | 58 |
| File uploads | Limited | Yes |
| Mobile recording | Limited | Yes |
| Free plan | 60 min/day noise cancellation, 7-day notes | 120 min/month, 3 min per recording |
| Biggest complaint | Support, lost notes | Charges after cancelling |

## Pricing head-to-head

| | Krisp Core | Notta Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $16 | About $13.99 |
| Billed yearly | $8/mo | $8.17/mo ($97.99/yr) |

On yearly billing they cost about the same. The choice comes down to what you need, not price.

## What users say about each

Krisp scores around 4.6–4.7 on G2, with praise centred on how well it removes noise. Its Trustpilot reviews are weaker, mostly about support. Notta averages about 4.4 on G2 and 4.1 on the App Store, but its Trustpilot page is dominated by one-star reviews about billing after cancellation.

## Which suits which job

- **Sales calls from a busy office:** Krisp.
- **Interviews for research or journalism:** Notta.
- **Meetings in Spanish, Japanese or mixed languages:** Notta.
- **Client calls where bots aren't welcome:** Krisp.

## Verdict

Krisp is the safer, more polished choice for live calls. Notta gives more transcription for the money and far wider language support, but only choose it if you're careful with billing. Both have free plans, so test your real use case before paying.
