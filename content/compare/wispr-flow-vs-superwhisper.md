## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without dictating in either app. We read Wispr's pricing, terms and data controls, Superwhisper's homepage and refund policy, and independent comparisons, many of which come from competitors. ## The short answer

Choose by where your words should go. If you want the easiest, cleanest dictation and you're comfortable with the cloud, pick Wispr Flow. If your words shouldn't leave your device, or you need to work offline, pick Superwhisper.

## Choose Wispr Flow if

- You want it to work well on day one.
- You move between devices through the day.
- You'd rather not choose models or settings.
- You want a team plan with admin controls and locked privacy settings.

## Choose Superwhisper if

- You need dictation with no internet connection.
- You handle confidential work.
- You prefer paying once.
- You enjoy configuring your tools.

## The real differences

**Where processing happens.** This is the core difference. Wispr says transcription always happens in the cloud, at any price. Superwhisper can run local models, and it says it works offline. It runs best on Apple Silicon Macs, and its FAQ says Intel Macs do better with cloud models.

**Polish.** Reviewers usually rate Wispr's clean-up highly, because it's tuned for that one experience. Superwhisper gives you more control, including using your own AI API keys, which also means more decisions to make.

**Privacy controls.** Wispr offers settings to turn off training, storage and context reading, but you're still trusting its cloud. Superwhisper's local mode removes that question, as long as you stay local. One Trustpilot reviewer did raise concerns about context reaching the cloud with a setting off, and another about an out-of-date privacy policy, so check it yourself if you use its cloud features.

**History.** Wispr had a public privacy row in 2025, covered in our [Wispr Flow review](/tools/wispr-flow). Rival blogs still use it heavily. Its current documents are much clearer.

**Platforms.** Wispr covers Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. Superwhisper's site now lists Windows, iOS, Android, Linux and Mac, though it began on Mac, and meetings are Mac only.

## Switching

Dictation apps hold very little you need to move. Rebuild your custom words and snippets, and that's about it. Wispr's terms won't refund the rest of a paid term, so switch near your renewal date. On Superwhisper, the refund window covers your first purchase, not renewals.

## Verdict

For comfort, Wispr Flow is the one to beat. For privacy, price and refund terms, Superwhisper wins, and a privacy-sensitive buyer shouldn't really consider anything else. Most people should try Superwhisper's free local mode first, because it costs nothing and may well be enough. If it feels like too much setup, try Wispr Flow's free plan, change its privacy settings, and see if the extra polish is worth $12 a month to you.
