## The short answer

They're good at different stages. Jenni helps while you're getting words on the page. Paperpal helps when you're improving text you've already written, especially for journal submission. If you only want one and English polish is your main need, pick Paperpal. If a blank page is your problem, pick Jenni.

## Choose Jenni if

- You're struggling to turn your reading into a first draft.
- You want sentence-by-sentence autocomplete.
- You want PDF chat and citations in one editor.
- Unlimited citations on a free plan appeal to you.

## Choose Paperpal if

- You've got a draft and need it polished.
- You're writing in English as a second language.
- You're preparing for journal submission.
- A strong review record matters to you.

## The real differences

**Drafting or polishing.** This is the core choice. Jenni's main feature is writing with you. Paperpal comes from the language-editing side, and is at its best on existing text.

**Integrity tools.** Paperpal advertises AI disclosure templates and an "AI Footprint" feature, which fits today's journal rules. Reviewers say Jenni has no built-in AI detector. For anyone who must disclose AI use, Paperpal's approach looks more helpful, though we haven't assessed how well those features work.

**Data use.** Both say they don't train AI on your content, Jenni on its teams page and Paperpal on its Trustpilot profile. Jenni's privacy policy adds a catch: document text can reach its analytics provider, and session replay can capture drafts unless you deny analytics cookies. We didn't read Paperpal's privacy policy, so don't assume it's free of similar issues.

**Scale.** Paperpal offers enterprise and institution plans. Jenni offers team plans with volume discounts. If your university already licenses one, use that.

## Academic integrity

The same rule applies to both. ICMJE asks authors to disclose AI use, says AI can't be an author, and holds humans responsible for what they submit. Your institution may be stricter, especially on drafting.

A tool that drafts for you raises harder questions than one that fixes your grammar. That's not a reason to avoid Jenni, but it's a reason to check first, and to be able to defend every sentence you submit. Check every citation too, in either tool.

## Switching

Moving is easy, because your work is ordinary text. Export your document and your references before you cancel. Note that Jenni's free plan limits export to body content, so export while you're still subscribed. Then cancel well before the renewal date, and keep the confirmation email.

## Verdict

For most people, we'd pick Paperpal, which is why it scores 8.1 against Jenni's 7.6. The reputation gap is large, and the polishing job carries less integrity risk. Pick Jenni if drafting is your real obstacle and your institution allows it. Start on its free plan, change your analytics setting, and pay monthly until you're sure.

## FAQ

### Is Jenni better than Paperpal?

For drafting, yes. For polishing and journal preparation, Paperpal is stronger.

### Which is cheaper?

They cost about the same on yearly plans, at around $140. Monthly, Paperpal is reported as a little cheaper.

### Which is safer for academic integrity?

Paperpal's polishing focus and disclosure templates carry less risk. Either way, follow your institution's rules.

### Do they train AI on my writing?

Both say they don't. Jenni's privacy policy does send some content to analytics, unless you deny those cookies.
