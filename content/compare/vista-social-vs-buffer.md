## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without first-hand use of our own. We read both pricing pages, Vista Social's terms and FAQ, and both Trustpilot profiles, including their one-star reviews. Checked November 5, 2026.

## The short answer

If you run fewer than about 10 channels, choose Buffer. It's cheaper, simpler, and has a free plan. If you manage 15 or more profiles and need reporting, an inbox and review management, Vista Social gives you more for the money. Weigh that against the Trustpilot warning below.

## Choose Vista Social if

- You manage many client or location profiles.
- You need a shared inbox, review management and client reports.
- Your team is small, so flat plans beat per-channel fees.

## Choose Buffer if

- You run a few channels, alone or in a small team.
- You mostly need to schedule posts and see simple analytics.
- You want a free plan rather than a trial.

## The real differences

**Scope.** Buffer is focused on publishing, with analytics and light engagement. Vista Social adds a fuller inbox, DM automations, review management, competitor reports and listening. If you won't use those, you'd be paying for extras.

**Pricing model.** Buffer charges per channel, so costs rise with every account. Vista Social charges in blocks, so you pay for capacity whether you use it or not.

**Users.** Buffer's Team plan includes unlimited team members. Vista Social includes two, three or four users, and doesn't publish what extras cost. For a large team on few channels, Buffer has a real advantage.

**Trust.** Buffer has a Trustpilot score of 4.3. Vista Social has none, because Trustpilot says it breached guidelines and that it removed fake reviews. Trustpilot doesn't say who wrote them, but buyers should know about it.

The crossover sits around 10 channels. Below that, Buffer wins clearly. Above it, Vista Social pulls ahead, more so if you need its extra features. Add $29 to the Vista Social column if you post to X.

## What users complain about in each

| | Vista Social | Buffer |
|---|---|---|
| Trustpilot score | None shown | 4.3 |
| Reviews | 78 | 153 |
| One-star share | 17% | 34% |
| Reviews in last 12 months | 26 | 63 |

Don't read the star shares at face value, because Vista Social's profile carries that warning.

The complaints differ usefully:

- **Vista Social:** billing and price rises (5 of 13), support (5), and cancelled or suspended accounts (4). None of those 13 reviews had a visible reply.
- **Buffer:** of the latest 20 one-star reviews, 8 describe scheduled posts failing, and 4 mention support. Only 6 of the 20 fall in the last 12 months, and Buffer replied to 5 of them, so its reliability complaints look less recent.

Scheduling failures are worth testing on your own networks. A missed client post is the sort of thing that costs an agency more than any plan does.

## What about Hootsuite?

Hootsuite's pricing page lists Standard at $99 per user with 10 social accounts, though it doesn't say clearly whether that's a monthly or yearly rate. That's the same headline price as Vista Social's Professional, with fewer accounts and one seat. Per-user pricing also means every new hire adds to the bill, so it suits a small team that wants an established name more than it suits a growing agency.

## Switching

Both tools connect directly to your social accounts, so moving is mostly reconnecting profiles and rebuilding queues. Export your reports and analytics first, as history may not move with you. If you're on an annual Vista Social plan, cancel at least 30 days before renewal.

## Verdict

For solo operators and small teams, Buffer is the better buy. It's simpler, cheaper, and you can start free.

For agencies with 15 or more profiles, Vista Social's flat plans and wider toolkit offer more, and it's the one we'd trial. Go monthly, test support, and decide with your own experience, given the question over its reviews.

## FAQ

### Is Vista Social cheaper than Buffer?

Only at scale. Below about 10 channels, Buffer costs less. Above that, Vista Social's flat plans usually win.

### Does Buffer have a free plan?

Yes, for up to 3 channels and 1 user, with 10 scheduled posts per channel.

### Which is better for agencies?

Vista Social, for client reports, inbox and review tools, though white label needs its $449 Scale plan.

### Why does Vista Social have no Trustpilot score?

Trustpilot says its rating is unavailable because of a guideline breach, and that it removed fake reviews.
