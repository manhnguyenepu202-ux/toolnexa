## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without generating models of our own. We read both pricing pages, both help centres, both sets of terms and both Trustpilot profiles, including the 20 latest one-star reviews for each. Checked November 5, 2026.

One warning first: we found no neutral quality test. Meshy ran the main print test itself, and the other comparisons are guides and blogs, so quality verdicts here are a guide only.

## The short answer

Tripo gives you more finished models for the same $20. Meshy has a stronger reputation, and it looks like the better fit for 3D printing. If volume decides it, choose Tripo. If you print or want the safer track record, choose Meshy.

## Choose Tripo if

- You need lots of assets on a small budget.
- Speed matters more than perfect detail.
- Auto-rigging is part of your workflow.

## Choose Meshy if

- You mainly 3D print and want slicer integrations.
- You'd like a longer, if conditional, refund window.
- Review history matters to your buying decision.

## The real differences

**Credits.** Tripo Pro includes 3,000 credits at $20. Meshy Pro includes 1,000 at the same price. Per-action costs are similar, so that gap flows straight through to output.

**Free plan.** Tripo gives 200 credits, with public, non-commercial models and limited exports. Meshy gives 100 credits and 10 downloads a month, only for untextured Meshy 6 Lite models, under CC BY 4.0. Tripo's is more useful for judging quality, but neither is a working plan.

**3D printing.** Meshy lists integrations with slicers such as Bambu Studio, Creality Print and OrcaSlicer, along with print repair tools. Its own test gave it 8 of 9 categories to Tripo's 5, and that test comes from Meshy, so read it with care.

**Training data.** Meshy's terms say it may use non-Enterprise customer content to improve its services, and you can opt out by email. Check Tripo's current policy if this matters, as we didn't confirm it.

**Rights.** Paid plans on both give you commercial use. Tripo's terms warn that other users may receive similar outputs, and Meshy's warn that outputs may not qualify for IP protection. So neither gives you exclusive protection.

We couldn't confirm a rigging cost for Meshy, so the rigged comparison isn't like for like. Pick the tier by counting your own monthly models, not by the headline figures.

Meshy's record is clearly stronger, though Trustpilot notes it asks customers for reviews, which tends to lift scores. Nearly all of Tripo's reviews are from the last year.

In both sets of 20 latest one-star reviews, billing comes first:

- **Tripo:** about 9 on cancelling and refunds, 6 on unexpected charges, and 6 on output quality.
- **Meshy:** about 11 on billing and cancelling, 7 on downloads blocked without paying, and 6 each on output and support.

That matters more than either score. Whichever you choose, know how to cancel before you pay, and check download rules before you generate.

## Switching

Both export common formats such as GLB and STL, so moving is easy. Download what you need before cancelling, because subscription credits disappear when a plan ends, and check licence terms for anything made on a free plan. Meshy's free outputs carry CC BY 4.0, which asks you to credit Meshy.

## Verdict

For most buyers making game assets, props and prototypes, Tripo is better value, and it's the one we'd try first.

For 3D printing, or if you're nervous about billing and want the stronger track record, Meshy is worth the extra cost. Neither replaces a 3D artist for hero characters.

Both have free tiers, so run the same reference image through each and inspect the meshes in Blender. That beats any scoreboard, including ours.

## FAQ

### Is Tripo cheaper than Meshy?

Per credit, yes. Both Pro plans cost $20, but Tripo includes 3,000 credits against Meshy's 1,000.

### Which is better for 3D printing?

Meshy looks stronger, with slicer integrations and repair tools, though the main test favouring it is its own.

### Which is better for game assets?

Tripo, for volume and rigging. Expect to clean up characters.

### Which has better reviews?

Meshy, at 4.7 from 4,009 Trustpilot reviews against Tripo's 4.3 from 510.
