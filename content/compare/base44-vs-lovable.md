## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without building apps of our own. We read pricing pages, docs and terms for both, and both Trustpilot profiles, including the 20 latest one-star reviews for each. Checked November 5, 2026.

## The short answer

For most founders, Lovable is the safer choice. It has much stronger reviews, Git sync on every plan, and credits that last longer. Base44 makes sense if you're non-technical, want everything bundled, and only need a simple app.

## Choose Base44 if

- You want the database, login and hosting handled in one place.
- You don't plan on hiring developers soon.
- You're testing an idea, and could rebuild it later.

## Choose Lovable if

- You may hand the project to a developer one day.
- You want unused credits to last beyond one month.
- Reputation matters to your buying decision.

## The real differences

**Exit path.** This is the big one. Lovable offers Git sync on all plans, including Free, and code download on paid plans. Base44 lists GitHub from Builder at $40, and while its FAQ says full source exports, reviewers report the backend and database are harder to move.

**Bundling.** Base44 includes more out of the box, and comparison guides say that's easier for non-coders. Lovable's output is often praised, but reviewers find its build commentary more technical.

**Credit life.** Base44's unused credits expire at reset. Lovable's monthly plan credits last two months, and top-ups last twelve.

**Team size.** Lovable says plans are priced by credits, not seats, and that workspaces support unlimited members on all plans.

Credits aren't equal between these tools, because each one prices work differently. So don't compare them directly. Run the same small feature in each free plan, and see what it uses.

The real comparison is the plan you'd actually need. Base44's cheapest plan with GitHub and backend functions is $40 on yearly billing. Lovable's $21 yearly plan already includes Git sync and code download. So for anyone who cares about an exit, Lovable is cheaper to start, even though Base44's headline price looks lower.

Base44's Monthly figure above is implied from its 20% yearly saving, so confirm it at checkout. Its pricing page defaults to Yearly, and several reviewers say they bought an annual plan by mistake.

## What one year costs

Here's what you'd pay up front on yearly billing, based on the plans above:

| Situation | Base44 | Lovable |
|---|---|---|
| Testing an idea, no code exit needed | Starter, $192 | Pro 100, $250 |
| You want your code in Git | Builder, $480 | Pro 100, $250 |
| About 200 to 250 credits a month | Builder, $480 | Pro 200, about $500 |

So Base44 wins on the first row and roughly ties on the third. The middle row is where it loses, and that's the row most serious projects end up on. Don't read this as a straight value win for Lovable, though, since one tool's credit buys a different amount of work than the other's.

We'd also avoid yearly billing on either tool until you've finished a real month of building. With non-refundable fees on both sides, paying monthly for a while is cheap insurance.

## Who should skip both

If your app handles payments, health data or anything regulated, neither tool removes the need for a developer's review. If you're a developer already, a coding assistant in your own editor will probably suit you better, because you keep full control of the stack from day one.

## What users complain about in each

| Reviews in last 12 months | 748 | 1,094 |
| Replies to negative reviews | 59% | 45% |

The gap here is large. Both profiles have recent reviews and enough volume to take seriously.

Among the latest 20 one-star reviews:

- **Base44:** support (about 16), billing and refunds (13), and cancelling or lock-in (about 7).
- **Lovable:** credit cost and burn (about 12), support (about 9), and bugs (about 9).

Put simply, Base44's unhappy users are upset about money and service, while Lovable's are more often upset about how quickly credits go. Both are real costs, but Lovable's is easier to manage.

## Switching

Moving off either tool takes work. Going from Lovable is usually easier, because your code is synced to your own repository. Going from Base44, plan for the data first. Export what you can, test whether your app runs elsewhere, and do it before renewal. Neither company refunds unused time.

## Verdict

Pick Lovable for most real projects, especially if the app might become a business. Pick Base44 for a quick test when you want the least setup, and accept that you may rebuild later.

If you're undecided, spend an hour on each free plan with the same idea. How easily you get unstuck will tell you more than any table.

## FAQ

### Is Base44 cheaper than Lovable?

Its headline price is lower, at $16 against $21 on yearly billing. But Base44's plan with GitHub starts at $40.

### Which is better for non-coders?

Base44 is often called easier, as it bundles everything and explains itself in plain language. Lovable isn't far behind.

### Can I leave Lovable or Base44 later?

Lovable's Git sync makes leaving simpler. With Base44, test your export, particularly the database.

### Which has better reviews?

Lovable, at 4.2 on Trustpilot against Base44's 2.7.
