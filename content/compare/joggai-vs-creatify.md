## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without making videos of our own. We read both pricing pages, JoggAI's credit guide and both sets of terms, then both Trustpilot profiles and the 20 latest one-star reviews for each. Comparisons from ngram and AdsGen come from rivals, so we give them less weight. Checked November 6, 2026.

## The short answer

Choose JoggAI if you want many avatar videos for little money. Choose Creatify if you run paid ads for a living, because its ad research and cloning tools do things JoggAI's plans don't list. Neither has a reassuring support record, so pay monthly and expect no refund on either.

## Choose JoggAI if

- Standard avatar videos are the bulk of your work.
- You need translations at low cost.
- You want credits that roll over for three months.
- You want API and app use from the same balance.

## Choose Creatify if

- Your job is testing paid ads on Meta or TikTok.
- You'd use competitor ad tracking and ad cloning.
- You want a very cheap way to start, at $10 to $15 a month.
- You value a bigger review history.

## The real differences

**Cost of avatar video.** This is where JoggAI stands out. Its Starter plan buys about 120 minutes of standard avatar video for $29. Creatify's Standard plan shows 11 of its 15-second Aurora avatar videos from 55 credits, or under 3 minutes, for $15.

**Ad tools.** Creatify's Pro plan adds a competitor ad tracker covering 10M+ Meta ads, Ad Clone, and ad agent automations. JoggAI has product photo, video and avatar tools, but not that research layer. That difference is why ngram, itself a rival to both, frames Creatify as product-led and JoggAI as avatar-led.

**AI actors.** Creatify advertises 300 actors on Standard and 1,500 on Pro. JoggAI's limits are framed around custom avatars, with 3 on Starter and 10 on Creator 1. Creatify's Pro includes 3 custom avatars.

**Video length.** JoggAI allows up to 30 minutes on Starter. Creatify allows 2 minutes on Standard and 10 from Pro. For ads, that rarely matters. For explainers, it does.

Creatify's record is stronger and much bigger. JoggAI's 33 reviews are too few to treat as a precise score, though they're still mostly bad.

The latest one-star reviews need more context, though. Creatify's 20 are all from August to October 2026, which suggests a recent run of problems. JoggAI's stretch from March 2025 to October 2026.

- **JoggAI:** support (8), credits (7), output quality (7), refunds (6), technical failures (6), cancelling (5).
- **Creatify:** output quality (13), refunds (12), support (12), cancelling (11), billing (10).

Creatify's recent pattern worries us. Several reviewers report annual renewals of around $500 to $600 that they didn't expect, then trouble getting help. So if you take a yearly plan on either tool, put the renewal date in your calendar on the day you sign up.

## Refunds and cancelling

Both say payments are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise, so neither has an advantage here. Creatify says you can cancel on the website or by emailing support. JoggAI documents an in-app route, and warns that deleting your account doesn't cancel your plan. Both keep your access until the period ends.

## A shared warning

Both tools are marketed for UGC-style ads, and that comes with legal risk. The FTC's rule bans testimonials from people who don't exist, including AI-generated ones. An avatar presenting your product is fine. An avatar claiming to be a happy customer isn't. Check your ad platform's rules on AI content too.

## Switching

Moving is simple, because finished videos are yours to keep and neither tool holds anything you need beyond your scripts. Custom avatars will need rebuilding. Use up your credits first, then cancel ahead of the renewal date.

## Verdict

If you're a small seller making lots of simple avatar videos, try JoggAI at $29 a month, because the cost per minute is hard to beat. If you're running paid ads as a job, Creatify is the better tool, and we scored it slightly higher, at 7.5 against 7.3. It's the ad research that earns that gap, not avatar value. Stay on monthly billing with either one.
