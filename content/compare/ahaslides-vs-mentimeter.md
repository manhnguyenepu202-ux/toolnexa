## The short answer

Choose by audience size. Under 100 people, AhaSlides is cheaper and gives you more on the free plan. Over 100 people, Mentimeter Basic costs less than AhaSlides Pro, with no participant cap. If you need custom branding at that size, AhaSlides Pro is back to being the better deal.

## Choose AhaSlides if

- You run sessions for under 100 people.
- You want a free plan that handles weekly sessions.
- You teach, because its education plans are far cheaper.
- You want branding without a $300 bill.

## Choose Mentimeter if

- You regularly present to hundreds of people.
- You want simple polls and word clouds with little setup.
- You'd rather have no participant limit to worry about.
- AhaSlides' 2026 breach rules it out for you.

## How we evaluated this

This is a research-based comparison, without running sessions in either tool. We read both pricing pages, both sets of terms, AhaSlides' security notice and help docs, both Trustpilot pages and Capterra's AhaSlides listing. AhaSlides publishes its own Mentimeter comparison, so we've treated claims from there as the vendor's view. Checked November 6, 2026.

## The real differences

**How participants are counted.** This decides most purchases. AhaSlides caps people connected at the same time: 50 on Free, 100 on Essential, 2,500 on Pro. Mentimeter's paid plans are unlimited. Its free plan allows 50 participants per month, plus unlimited participants once a month.

**Free plan usefulness.** For a weekly class of 30, AhaSlides' free plan can keep working, slide limits permitting. Mentimeter's 50 monthly participants would go quickly. AhaSlides' free plan also includes PowerPoint import, while Mentimeter lists import under Basic.

**Slide import.** AhaSlides says imported slides stay editable and that Mentimeter turns them into static images. That's its own claim, so test it with your own file if you're relying on it.

**Activities.** AhaSlides claims more types, including games and self-paced quizzes. Mentimeter's reputation rests on being simple, and that's what its Trustpilot reviewers praise most.

**Trust.** AhaSlides disclosed a breach in September 2026 affecting email, invoice and hashed password data for all AhaSlides.com accounts. See our [AhaSlides review](/tools/ahaslides) for the detail. Mentimeter's issues are about billing. Recent Trustpilot complaints describe auto-renewals, refused refunds and support that didn't answer.

## What users say about each

AhaSlides has a thin Trustpilot record, with 7 reviews and a 3.1 score, but it does well on Capterra at 4.6 from 67 reviews. Its rating for value there is 4.4.

Mentimeter has 67 Trustpilot reviews, 57 from the last year, and shows 3.7. It's a split picture, with 58% five-star and 31% one-star. Praise covers ease and engagement. Complaints cover billing terms, design limits, and a participant feedback survey that one reviewer says paying customers can't turn off.

Trustpilot flags both companies for not replying to negative reviews. G2 shows both at about 4.7 in search snippets, though we couldn't open it.

## Refunds and cancelling

Both offer 14 days, and both exclude you if you've already used the product at a live event, though Mentimeter states that in its plans FAQ, not its terms. So neither is really a trial. Use the free plan for that.

Mentimeter's terms are clear about renewals: no refund once a new period has begun. Complaints suggest people miss this, so set a reminder. AhaSlides' terms conflict with its own FAQ on refunds and on what happens at cancellation, so save copies of your content.

## Switching

There's not much to move. Polls and quizzes need rebuilding, but they're short. Export your results first, which takes a paid plan on both. If you're on a Mentimeter yearly plan, wait until close to renewal, then cancel before it runs.

## Verdict

AhaSlides is our pick for teachers, trainers and anyone under 100 people. It costs less, and its free plan is much more usable. Mentimeter is the pick for large audiences on a budget, and for anyone who'd rather avoid a vendor with a fresh breach. If you go with AhaSlides, use a unique password. If you go with Mentimeter, put the renewal date in your calendar.

For small groups and classrooms, we think so, mainly on price. For large crowds, Mentimeter Basic is the better buy.
